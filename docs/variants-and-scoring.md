# Variants, upgrades and scoring

> Read this when you touch variants, upgrades, the upgrade ceremony, value / score / leaderboard maths, scoring settings or holo masking.

## Variants, upgrades & leaderboard

Packs can opt in to **variants**: every card can be pulled as Regular / Reverse Holo / Full Art / Shiny Full Art, and viewers upgrade duplicates in the Twitch panel. Season 1's pack has `upgradesEnabled: false`, so it behaves exactly as before.

- **Entry encoding (`lib/variants.ts`)**: a collection entry is `cardNumber + 1_000_000 × variantIndex` (regular = plain number, so all legacy data is valid). Always go through `encodeEntry` / `decodeEntry`; card numbers must stay < 1,000,000. `public/widget.js` and `public/twitch-panel/panel.js` mirror the constant (no build step there), keep in sync. `countPulls()` / `uniqueCount()` decode, so "a card" is one card regardless of variant.
- **Config**: `Pack.upgradesEnabled`, `variantWeights` (default 100/25/5/1, relative, rolled *after* the card, normalised), `upgradeCosts` (5 regular→reverse, 3 reverse→full, 3 full→shiny). `Card.upgradesEnabled` (undefined/true = follow pack; false opts the card out) and `Card.fullArtImageUrl` (art for Full Art + Shiny). Reverse Holo and Shiny are the holo effect on the Regular / Full Art image. There is no separate holo art. Effective check: `upgradesAllowed(pack, card)`. All edited in admin (pack settings, card editor).
- **Upgrades**: strictly one step up, same card, same variant, active season only; no downgrades. `lib/upgrade.ts` → `POST /api/twitch/upgrade` (JWT → opaque ID → username; dev mode accepts any bearer).
- **Upgrade ceremony**: a successful upgrade plays an animation. The spent copies spiral into the middle, a flash and ring, then the new variant is revealed with its holo / glow (sparkles for Full Art and Shiny), its label, the cost and (on the website) the projected points. Website: `UpgradeCeremony.tsx` (portal, `.ug-*` rules in `globals.css`), played by `UpgradeButton` when it is given the card's art (`card` prop); the page refreshes when the ceremony closes, not before, because the upgraded row may vanish and cut it short. Twitch panel: `playUpgradeCeremony()` in `panel.js` with the same rules copied into `panel.css` (the stage is designed at 340×520 and scaled to fit the small frame). Both use **the `to` variant and `cost` the server returns** (not "next on the ladder"), since a switched-off variant is skipped. Reduced motion skips the movement and shows the result; click / Esc / Enter closes after ~1.8s, and it closes itself after 9s. **Holo is the Overlay Editor's holo, everywhere.** The shimmer on the website, in the upgrade ceremony and in the Twitch panel is driven by the SAME per-variant settings as the overlay (Overlay Editor → Variants → Holographic shimmer: on / off / auto by the pack's min-stars, colours, strength, sweep time, angle, blend, loop; global values with the pack's overrides on top). `lib/holo.ts` (`getHoloResolver()`, server) resolves them per card into a `HoloSpecs` ({ on, style: CSS variables } per variant, built by `buildHoloSpecs` in the client-safe `lib/holo-core.ts`, which mirrors `applyLook` in `widget.js`); pages pass it to the art (`holoClass()` + `holoVars()`, `.holo-fx`), and `/api/twitch/season` sends it on every card for `panel.js` (`applyHolo()`). The CSS (`globals.css`, `panel.css`) keeps the overlay's geometry. An 8-band gradient tile 5 card-widths wide and tall, sliding 8 card-widths in one sweep (a loop slides exactly one tile), clipped to the art's silhouette with `--art-mask`. `.holo-live` sweeps by itself (detail views, the ceremony's reveal card and spent copies); grid tiles sweep on hover only. The glow / rocking (`.vfx-full`, `.vfx-shiny`) are separate from the holo. If you add a holo property, add it in `buildHoloSpecs` and the CSS too.
- **Switching variants off**: `Pack.disabledVariants` and `Card.disabledVariants` (Reverse / Full / Shiny; **Regular can never be switched off**, `cleanDisabled()` enforces it). Always go through the helpers in `lib/variants.ts`: `allowedVariants(pack, card?)` (pack and card switches combine), `effectiveWeights(pack, card?)` (switched-off weights zeroed, used by every odds display and `variantMultipliers`) and `upgradeStep(pack, card, from)`. An upgrade goes to the next variant that is **on**, and skipping a switched-off one costs the **product of every step crossed** (regular → full with Reverse off = reverse × full copies), so skipping is never a shortcut. The overlay (`rollVariant(weights, off)`) never rolls a switched-off variant; the Twitch panel gets `pack.disabledVariants` and each card's allowed `variants` from `/api/twitch/season` and mirrors the step maths in `panel.js`; the collection page precomputes `variants` / `steps` per card. Admin: pack-level switches in Pack Settings and the wizard's Upgrades step; per-card switches in the card editor, the bulk card editor (chips) and **inline on the pack page** (`CardToggles`: a per-card upgrades switch plus variant chips, saved immediately), a "Variants & odds" panel (pack-level switches with the resulting odds) and **Variant tools** (`bulkCards`: switch off Full Art & Shiny where a card has no full-art image, upgrades on/off for all, clear per-card switches). Server-rendered forms carry `variant_on_*` checkboxes plus a `variant_switches=1` marker; a form without the marker never touches the stored switches. `scripts/check-upgrades.mts` covers all of this.
- **Locking**: the collection is one JSON blob per season. Every read-modify-write goes through `withCollectionLock` / `addCollectionEntry` in `lib/kv.ts` (Redis `SET NX EX`). `POST /api/collection` with a `pull` now **appends server-side** (the overlay's in-memory copy goes stale after upgrades/grants and used to overwrite them) and returns `{ owned }`, which the widget adopts.
- **Grants**: webhook (`variant` in the body, default regular), admin quick action and admin user page all take a variant.
- **Scoring settings (Admin → Settings → Scoring)**: every scoring number is a field of `ScoringSettings` in `lib/scoring-core.ts` (pure, client-safe: shape, defaults, limits, `sanitizeScoring`; `lib/scoring.ts` re-exports it and adds the KV load / save), stored in KV (`scoring:settings`), clamped by `sanitizeScoring()` (ranges in `SCORING_LIMITS`), and defaulting to the original behaviour (`DEFAULT_SCORING`): `baseScore` (10), `rarityExponent` (1), `serialBonusMax` (3) and `serialBonusCutoff` (20), `upgradeMargin` (1.2), `completionBonusPct` (25), `completeSetBonusPct` (50), `leaderboardSize` (100). They are **global**, changing them re-scores every season immediately (values are always computed live; nothing is stored per season). `getSeasonLeaderboard()` loads them and `SeasonValues.settings` carries them to everything downstream (`collectionBonuses`, `projectUpgradeGain`, the FAQ numbers, My Stats). The pure functions in `lib/leaderboard.ts` take `settings` as a parameter defaulting to `DEFAULT_SCORING`, so they stay testable (`scripts/check-upgrades.mts`). Add new tunables to `ScoringSettings` + `DEFAULT_SCORING` + `SCORING_LIMITS` and a `ScoringField` in the settings page.
- **Value & leaderboard (`lib/leaderboard.ts`)**: per-card value = `baseScore (10) × rarity^rarityExponent × variantMultiplier` (`CardValue.values[variant]`, no longer depends on anyone's collection). rarity = 1/(pull rate × cards in season); variant multiplier = max(odds ratio, previous × cost × 1.2). **There is no scarcity any more.** Instead each *copy* earns a **serial bonus** from its number in the card ledger (`lib/serials.ts`; the serial counts per card AND version, so Shiny #1 is the first Shiny): `serialMultiplier(n, settings)` in `lib/scoring-core.ts` = `1 + (serialBonusMax − 1) × (1 − (n − 1) / serialBonusCutoff)` for `n ≤ serialBonusCutoff`, else 1 (cutoff 0 = off). A copy's points are `copyPoints(cv, variant, serial, settings)` = `max(1, round(raw × multiplier))` (`cv.maths.raw`, the unrounded value; no serial = `cv.values`). The bonus belongs to the copy: trades move it to the new holder, upgrades spend the **newest** serials first and mint the next free serial of the new version, copies in escrow score nothing. `getSeasonLeaderboard()` loads the ledger once with `loadSerialIndex(collectionName)` (`SerialIndex` = owner → entry → sorted serials, plus the minted counter; built by the pure `buildSerialIndex` in `lib/serials-core.ts`) and `SeasonValues.serials` carries it; `pointsFor(values, entries, username)` is the one place that adds up a viewer's copies (rows, `scoreFor`, upgrade projection). **A collection whose copies are not numbered yet (`backfillSerials` not run) has `serials = null` and no bonuses**, run "Number existing copies" first; backfilled numbers are estimates (`est`) but score the same. Because nobody else's copies move a value, the My Stats "± pts" (`projectUpgradeGain(values, owned, username, card, from, to, cost)`) is just that viewer's own before/after (spent low serials can make it negative). Never re-promise "always a net gain". **Trade prices include the bonus**: `TradeCardInfo` carries `raw` + `bonus` (the rule), `stackPoints(items, cards, pool?)` in `lib/trade-view-core.ts` prices a stack from a `SerialPool` (entry → serials) taking the **highest** serials first (trades move the newest copies); `getTradePools(season, trades, viewer)` supplies each open offer's escrow (its give side, `SerialIndex.escrow`) and the viewer's own holdings (what an offer asks of them), `poolsFor(trade, pools)` picks the right ones (a viewer's own offer has no pool for what it asks), and `TradeCard`, `matchTrade` / `bestMatches` and the admin trades page take them. The composer prices your side from `minePool` and theirs from `GET /api/trades/holdings` (`pool`); the board's "wants in return" side for a listing you do not yet fill stays at plain values. The activity feed / Discord points for trade events (`announceTrade`) are priced too: the engine reads the serials on each side BEFORE the copies move (`snapshotPools` in `lib/trades.ts`: the escrow for what is given, the taker's holdings for what is asked on an accept; `serialPool` in `lib/serials.ts`), queues them with the event (`EventPools`), and `announceTrade` prices with them. The bell notices themselves quote no points. `scripts/check-trade-points.mts` (mock KV) covers it. **My Stats** (`computeUserStats`) reports `serialPoints` (its own line in the points breakdown, here and on the profile) and lists copies that earn a bonus one by one in "Most valuable copies" with their `#serial`. Per-card displays that add `count × values[v]` are base values; the profile card dialog adds `GridCard.serial.points` (from `serialFor` in `lib/profile-data.ts`) to its total. Score = sum over every copy owned **plus two collection bonuses** (`collectionBonuses()` in `lib/leaderboard.ts`), both measured against the *set value* (what one Regular copy of every card is worth right now): a **completion bonus** of `completionBonusPct` (default 25%) × set value × the share of the set owned, and a **complete-collection bonus** of `completeSetBonusPct` (default 50%) × set value once the viewer owns at least one of every card (duplicates / better versions don't count towards completeness). `LeaderboardRow` carries `cardPoints` (serial bonuses included), `serialPoints` (just the serial part), `completionBonus`, `setBonus`, `complete` alongside the total `score`; `scoreFor()` (used by `projectUpgradeGain`) includes the bonuses. The leaderboard shows a 🧀 Complete chip, My Stats a points breakdown, the FAQ the live numbers. Per season. Surfaces: `/leaderboard[/seasonId]`, `GET /api/twitch/leaderboard`, a Leaderboard tab in the panel, a rank/"Full leaderboard" link on the profile, and links from the admin dashboard (header button + Top Collectors panel). The leaderboard page opens with a "How are points worked out?" expandable at the top (`?how=1` opens it; the collection card details link there).
- **Holo masking**: the holographic shimmer must sit *exactly* on the art and follow its silhouette (the card art is 600x891 with transparent rounded corners). On the overlay, `.holographic-overlay` is a child of `.main-card` (`inset: 0`, so it follows the art's pop / rock transforms) and is clipped with `mask-image: var(--card-mask)`. Set in `widget.js` to the shown art's **static thumbnail** (same shape, tiny, never animated; full-art thumbnail for Full Art / Shiny). On the website and panel the `.vfx-reverse` / `.vfx-shiny` `::after` sheen uses `--art-mask` the same way (`CardTile`, the detail art, `setArtMask()` in `panel.js`). Don't move the overlay back outside the card or size it with fixed offsets (it used to be 21px too wide and bled past the corners).
- **Overlay effects**: `variant-reverse|full|shiny` classes on the theater wrapper (`widget.css`). **Shiny cards rock gently left to right** everywhere they appear (`.vfx-shiny` on the site and panel, `variantRock` on the overlay) using the standalone `rotate` property so it layers on hover/pop transforms; disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Test panel (`?dev`, see below): pick pack / card / variant, draw several, or draw all four variants of a card.
- `npx tsx scripts/check-upgrades.mts` sanity-checks encoding, roll odds, and the value maths.

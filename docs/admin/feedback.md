# Feedback inbox and deletion requests

> Read this when you touch the feedback inbox or account deletion requests.

## Feedback inbox (/admin/feedback, /mod/feedback)
Suggestions and problem reports from viewers, one inbox (`lib/feedback-core.ts` pure, `lib/feedback.ts` store). Items are `feedback:<id>` documents, `feedback:list` holds the ids newest first (capped at 1000) and `feedback:new` the ids still waiting for staff (so the nav badge, the mod bar badge and the dashboard Needs attention line are one SCARD). Staff set a status (new / planned / done / won't do / duplicate) and write a reply with `updateFeedbackAction` (`app/admin/feedback-actions.ts`, **mods may use it**, so it is in `DOCUMENTED_MOD_ACTIONS`); the sender gets a bell / push notice of kind `feedback` (own Settings switch `notifyFeedback`, on by default; push group "Messages from the admins"). The staff login is kept on the item (`repliedBy`) and never shown to the sender or in Discord. `listFeedback` first sweeps: items resolved more than 90 days ago are deleted (open ones never). The Discord category `feedback` (off on existing channels, preset "Feedback") posts each new item with the sender's login only, Markdown-escaped and cut to 500 characters, silent in a sandbox. **User reports (ROADMAP 17.2):** kind `report` with `target` (login) and `reason` (`offensive` / `cheating` / `scam` / `other`), details in `body`, an optional trade / print link in `from`. Filed from the profile's Share menu (`ReportDialog`, `POST /api/feedback/report`: signed in, not staff, Turnstile, 5 a day on its own counter `feedback:rate:report:<u>:<day>`, one open report per reporter and target, no self-reports, the target must be a known collector). The reporter is only ever visible to staff; the Discord embed names the target and the reason only (not the details, not the reporter) and goes only to channels whose `feedbackKinds` include `report`. Closing one tells the reporter the outcome only (`noticeText`), never the staff reply, which is a staff note. `/admin/feedback` and `/mod/feedback` have a Reports filter with a waiting count, the dashboard's Needs attention lists waiting reports, and each card links the target's profile, user page and the trade. Actions are the existing ones. The reporter's own reports are in their data export (not reports about them). `scripts/check-feedback.mts` covers validation, the daily limit, notices, the sweep, Discord gating and the export.

**Account deletion requests (ROADMAP 11.3)**: a viewer asks from the bottom of Settings (`DeleteAccount`, typed login, Turnstile, `POST /api/account/deletion`; `DELETE` cancels while it waits). The request is an inbox item of kind `deletion` (`requestDeletion`, `cancelDeletion`, `closeDeletion` in `lib/feedback.ts`; not counted in the daily send limit, one open at a time) with its own filter and a red count. Only **admins** handle it (`deleteViewerForRequestAction`, `declineDeletionAction`, and `updateFeedbackAction` refuses a deletion item for mods): **Delete this viewer** runs `deleteViewerAccount` (`lib/account-deletion.ts`, also used by the Users page delete): open offers cancelled first (escrowed prints come back), the restore point (14 days, as the Privacy Policy says), every collection emptied (the print ledger retires what they held), profile, settings (pinned, favourites, watch index), notices, alert devices, score history, achievements, hidden flag, their other feedback and the extension's opaque-ID links (a key scan, skipped in a sandbox); the deletion record is kept and marked done without a notice. **Decline** needs a reason and notifies the sender. A request waiting more than 30 days (`DELETION_OVERDUE_DAYS`) turns the dashboard Needs attention line and the card red. `scripts/check-account-deletion.mts`.

**Items about staff (ROADMAP 51.6)**: an item "names" a staff member when it is a report whose `target` is a staff login, or when its title, body or expected text mentions a staff login as a whole word (case-insensitive; staff = the staff list plus the fixed super admins; the sender is never counted as named). `staffNamedIn` (`lib/feedback-core.ts`) works this out **when the item is read**, not when it is created, and sets `aboutStaff` on the returned copy; nothing is stored, so older items are covered with no migration and a newly added staff member's old mentions count. Every mod reader passes a `FeedbackReader` (`{ login, admin }`) and gets the rule applied on the server: `listFeedback(filter, reader)` (the mod Feedback page), `countNewFeedback(reader)` (the mod bar badge; a non-admin count is worked out from the visible items so it cannot hint that a hidden one exists), and `getFeedbackFor(id, reader)` (`updateFeedbackAction` does nothing for an item that names the mod). A mod never sees an item that names them, in the list, the count or by acting on its id. Admins see everything, including items about themselves (nobody is above admin), with a red "About staff" chip in `FeedbackInbox`, and `/admin/feedback` lists those items first. Admin-only readers (`/admin/feedback`, the dashboard) pass no reader. Not covered: the Discord `feedback` channel post for a report still names the target, so a mod who can read that channel sees it there. `scripts/check-feedback.mts` covers the matching, the per-reader lists, the id lookup and the counts.

# Review batch P7: Words

Pages: `/faq`, `/privacy`, `/terms`. Reviewed 2026-10-15 against the code as it is now. This is a wording and accuracy pass by a developer, not legal advice: the Privacy Policy and Terms should still be read by whoever is accountable for them.

## Method

- Read the rendered text of all three pages in full (every FAQ question expanded) and checked each number, name and promise against the code: the version odds, upgrade costs, point formula and bonuses (these are filled in live from the current season and scoring settings), the trade limits (live from the trade settings), the Turnstile pass, the retention caps (activity log, finished trades, bell notices, score samples, restore points, profile pictures, the login cookie), the saved-alert and Looking for limits, the show-off cooldown, the privacy behaviour (private profile, admin-hidden collectors), the staff and moderation model, the backups, and the stored data categories.
- Checked the Cheesygidds-only rule is stated in the FAQ ("Only on Cheesygidds's Twitch channel. It doesn't run anywhere else"), the Terms (1) and the Privacy Policy (opening), and the footer.
- Sweep of the three pages at 390, 768 and 1280 px: clean. Deep links to FAQ sections and questions checked in a browser.

## Findings

### Fixed

- **Terms, section 1: a broken sentence** ("... and, in seasons that enable it. Can be upgraded into rarer variants."). Now one sentence.
- **FAQ called the page "Collections"**, the nav and footer now say "Collectors" (P1).
- **FAQ and Terms did not match the trading and upgrade behaviour**: Accept / Take / Decline / Cancel and Upgrade now ask for a second tap ("Sure?"); the FAQ says so. The Terms said trading happens "on the website"; it also happens in the Twitch Extension.
- **FAQ: the show-off command was limited to "once every few seconds"**; it is every 15 seconds (now taken from the constant).
- **FAQ: ended seasons**: it now says a season's leaderboard shows the final standings saved when it ended, with its awards (P4).
- **Privacy Policy, security section said "administration is behind a password"**; it is limited to staff who sign in with their Twitch account, with an emergency password as a fallback.
- **Privacy Policy, data it did not list**: the labels and restrictions a moderator or the channel owner can put on a profile (left off the boards, not trading); the profile layout and binder, a pinned copy, earned achievements and the newest bell notices; the preferences the website keeps in the browser's local storage (collection layout, rank movement, trade animations seen); and the retention of bell notices (newest 30), score samples (newest 120 per season) and the 14-day restore point kept before a moderator or the channel owner resets, merges or deletes someone's cards.
- **Deep links to a closed FAQ question** (for example `/faq#captcha`, `/faq#not-nfts`) landed on a closed question, and one did not scroll to it at all. A small client component (`OpenOnHash`) opens the question the address points at, and any question around it, and scrolls to it, on load and when the hash changes.

### Checked and fine

The version odds (76 / 19 / 3.8 / 0.8 %), the upgrade costs (5 / 3 / 3, so 15 and 45), the serial bonus (3x at #1 fading to #20), the collection bonuses (25% and 50% of the set value, 472 points in the review data), the per-card value table (draft packs left out since P2), the trade limits (48 hours, 5 open, 20 seconds, 8 stacks, all live), the retention numbers quoted for the activity log (500), finished trades (400), profile pictures (7 days), the login cookie (30 days), the Turnstile pass (15 minutes), saved alerts (10), the private-profile description, the backups line (14 days), the Cheesygidds-only rule, and "no NFTs / no real-world value" in the FAQ and Terms. No em dashes or emoji in the three pages.

### Improvements (for you to pick)

1. **`LEGAL_UPDATED` stays `15 October 2026`**: the legal text changed, but that date is already today's (set when the backup line was added). It needs a later date the next time the Privacy Policy or Terms change on another day.
2. **The contact line falls back to "contact the streamer through the stream's Twitch channel"** until `LEGAL_OPERATOR_NAME`, `LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL` and `LEGAL_GOVERNING_LAW` are set in Vercel Production (the Phase 0 checklist). Check `/privacy` and `/terms` on production after setting them: the operator name, contact address and governing law should read properly in place of the neutral wording.
3. **FAQ claims the code cannot confirm**: the stream commands and rules ("Use the !pack command...", "Follow and subscribe..., both are ways to earn packs", the show-off command's name) describe how the stream is run today. They are the streamer's to keep true; a short "ask in chat" fallback is already there.

### Panel

The FAQ describes the Twitch extension's Trades tab and extras as they are in the deployed panel; the new second-tap behaviour is on the website only, and the panel already asked first. Nothing to change here while the freeze is on.

## Guard against drift

`scripts/check-words.mts` (part of `npm run check`) reads the three pages and the code and fails when a quoted number disagrees with its constant (activity, trade history, notices, score samples, restore points, avatars, cookie lifetime, the Turnstile pass, saved alerts), when the channel-only rule goes missing from the FAQ, Terms or Privacy Policy, when an em dash appears, or when the two fixed wordings above come back.

## Not verified here

Legal adequacy, the streamer's own chat commands, the Twitch extension's text in the Twitch developer console (`twitch-extension/SUBMISSION.md`), and how the three pages read to someone new to the game (that wants a real reader).

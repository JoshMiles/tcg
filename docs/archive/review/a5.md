# Review batch A5: trades and serials

Pages: `/admin/trades` (Overview, Offers & log, Controls & settings), `/admin/serials` (overview grid, a card's copies, a holder's copies) and `/admin/serials/[seasonId]/[copyId]`. Seeded with `scripts/seed-review.mts`, swept with `scripts/sweep-admin.mjs` at 390, 768 and 1280 px, then walked by hand in a browser (log rows and detail panel, extend all, close and reopen trading, cancel and refund by scope, settings save, the serials views, giving a copy to another viewer, the remove dialog), and loaded again with the browser in other time zones.

## Sweep

All pages clean at the three widths. The duplicate-key warning the tooling run noted on `/admin/serials` no longer appears. An unknown copy id answers 200 with the shared not-found screen (no `h1`, for A8).

## Bugs (fixed)

1. **Hydration mismatch for visitors outside UTC.** Client components that print a date in the browser's own time zone (`toLocaleString('en-GB')` with no `timeZone`) render different text on the server (UTC) and in the browser. M1 fixed the locale half; the zone half was still there. Reproduced on `/admin/activity` with the browser set to New York ("A tree hydrated but some attributes ... didn't match"); it would have shown on production for anyone not on UTC. Fixed in every client component that formats a date: the activity feed and its detail panel, the trade log, the users list, the user panels, the Unused assets panel, the Overlay Editor's versions, and on the public site the score chart and the Card Dex. All now print UTC. `scripts/check-locale.mts` now also fails when a client component formats a date with a locale but no `timeZone`. Re-checked in New York and Tokyo across the admin, mod and public pages: no mismatches.
2. **The Serials page counted draft packs' cards** ("Cards: 25 in this season", and the card picker listed them) although a draft pack is never drawn; it is 20 now, the same set the leaderboard uses.

## Safer actions (fixed)

- **Cancel & refund** (bulk, by scope) used `window.confirm`; it is the standard dialog now, off while there is nothing to cancel or no viewer chosen.
- **Give this copy to another viewer** was one click after typing a name. It is a confirmation that says whose copy it is, that the serial bonus moves with it, that it is logged, and that an unused name becomes a new account (a typo is visible).
- `ConfirmButton` gained a `disabled` option for forms whose fields are not ready.

## Visuals (fixed)

- The copy page's tab names the copy (`Copy 1.0.1 · Admin`); times on the serials pages say UTC; the trade stats note uses a fixed date format.

## Checked and fine

The log (3 rows, filters, detail panel with Cancel & refund and Block from trading), Extend all, Close and Reopen trading per season, cancel by scope with its dialog, the settings form (limits come from `TRADE_LIMITS`, sanitised on the server), the overview grid and per-card and per-holder views, the copy page's history, Remove (dialog), giving a copy (the history and both collections stay in step).

## Not verified

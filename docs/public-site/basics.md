# Public site basics

> Read this when you touch branding, theme, install, the FAQ, the changelog page, legal pages, the top bar and search, SEO, share images, error pages, the leaderboard cache.

## Public site (The Cheesy TCG)
The product is branded **The Cheesy TCG** (icon: `public/brand/icon.png`, also `app/icon.png` for the favicon; the iOS home-screen icon is `public/apple-touch-icon-v3.png` (a new file name busts iOS's per-URL cache; the root `apple-touch-icon.png` and `-precomposed.png` stay for iOS's direct probes) and is also listed in the manifest; `BrandMark` renders it). Every public page sits inside `SiteShell` (`app/components/site/`: sticky `SiteNav` with a mobile menu, plus a footer with Privacy/Terms). Existing sections get it from a small `layout.tsx` (`collection`, `leaderboard`, `privacy`, `terms`); the new pages live in the `app/(site)/` route group, which shares the same shell. The admin area keeps its own layout and password login.
- Theme: the dark card-table look plus cheese accents, `.cheese-hero`, `.cheese-holes`, `.rind`, `.cheese-float` in `globals.css`. daisyUI 5 is loaded with one theme, `cheesy` (the same navy, yellow and poke colours, `color-scheme: dark`). Shared primitives are daisyUI: `btn` (a `btn btn-primary` with `rounded-xl`, `rounded-lg` or `rounded-full` is the 3D yellow button), `card` (navy surface, blue rim and card shadow), `input`, chips as `badge`, and the admin dialog as a `modal`. Admin, profile, binder, the card dex, the leaderboard, the season-stats card table, the public trade pages, account, settings, notifications and feedback use those primitives. The cheese `.rind` sections are still hand-written.
- **Install the app**: `/install` (`InstallGuide`: steps for iPhone / Android / computer, opens on the visitor's device via `lib/install-detect.ts`), a phone-only dismissible hint on the home page (`InstallHint`), a footer link and an **install icon in the top bar** (`InstallButton` in `SiteNav`). The browser's one-shot `beforeinstallprompt` is caught early by `app/components/site/install-store.ts` (loaded with the page's scripts, before hydration) and shared: the icon opens the browser's own install dialog where there is one (Chrome, Edge, Android), otherwise links to `/install` (iPhone, iPad and Android browsers without a prompt); it is hidden once installed (standalone), inside another app's browser and on a computer whose browser can't install. **Pull to refresh (installed app only):** PullToRefresh (mounted in SiteShell) listens for touch drags when isStandalone(); from scrollY === 0, a drag down shows a refresh indicator under the top bar, and releasing past 70px of resisted pull calls 
outer.refresh() (server data, keeps client state). It ignores drags that start in a [role=dialog], a form field or a scroller not at its top. With prefers-reduced-motion (or data-motion=reduce) there is no rotation or spring. The More sheet has a matching **Refresh** row (RefreshRow), shown only when installed. The manifest, icons and offline page are in `app/manifest.ts`, `public/icons/` and `public/sw.js`. The manifest lists three phone `screenshots` (`public/screenshots/install-1..3.png`, 780x1688, `form_factor: 'narrow'`: home, Card Dex, a profile) so Chrome on Android shows the bigger install sheet. They were captured from the seeded local site (`scripts/seed-review.mts`, 390x844 at 2x, cookie notice dismissed), never production; retake them the same way when those pages change a lot. The iPhone launch screen (no white flash) is `metadata.appleWebApp.startupImage` in `app/layout.tsx`: one portrait PNG per iPhone screen size in `public/splash/` (the mascot, a third of the width, centred on `#0a0f1e`), made by `scripts/make-pwa-icons.mjs`; the sizes live in `lib/iphone-screens.json`; add a new iPhone size there and rerun the script. **Safe areas:** the root viewport is `viewport-fit=cover`, so the sticky top bar, the footer and the full-screen public dialogs (class `safe-inset` in `globals.css`) pad by `env(safe-area-inset-*)` to stay clear of the notch and home indicator (0 in a normal browser, so desktop is unchanged); in standalone (`display-mode: standalone`) the body has `overscroll-behavior-y: none`. `theme-color`, the manifest `background_color` and `theme_color` are all the dark navy `#0a0f1e`, matching the top bar.
- **FAQ upkeep**: when a public feature changes, update the FAQ in the same PR (it now has Your profile, serial numbers (`#serials`), trading matches / notices, privacy download and shortcuts), plus Privacy / Terms when data or rules change (serials, settings, score history are listed there). Bump `LEGAL_UPDATED` when you do.
- **Changelog**: `/changelog` ("What's new", indexed) lists the generated entries followed by the hand-written history in `lib/changelog.ts` (`{ date, title, items, tags?, pr?, audience? }`, written for players). Generated entries come from the `## Changelog` section of each merged PR: `scripts/build-changelog.mjs` runs before `next build`, reads merged PRs into main through the GitHub API (`GITHUB_CHANGELOG_TOKEN`, fine-grained, Pull requests: read; owner / repo from `VERCEL_GIT_REPO_OWNER` / `VERCEL_GIT_REPO_SLUG`, else the git remote) and writes the gitignored `.next-changelog.json`. It never fails a build: no token, no network or an API error writes `[]`. Parsing lives in `scripts/changelog-parse.mjs`: the section stops at the next heading, a `---` or a line starting with the robot emoji or "Generated with"; `Title:` on the first line overrides the PR title; an `Audience:` line (any of players, admin, mod; `staff` means admin and mod, `both` / `all` mean all three; default players, and the entry's `audience` field is left out when it is just players) splits the entries into `CHANGELOG` (public), `ADMIN_CHANGELOG` and `MOD_CHANGELOG`; `/admin/changelog` shows them as Admin / Mod / Players tabs (`?tab=`), with an Edit link to each PR on entries that have one, and `/mod/changelog` shows `MOD_CHANGELOG`; a `Tags:` line (new / improved / fixed) or the same PR labels become the pills; only the bulleted list is published (prose around it is skipped, the first plain line after it ends it) and markdown is turned into plain text; `none` on the first line or no section leaves the PR out, and so does a PR whose title starts with ROADMAP; items are capped at 500 characters; commit messages are never read; any line or title naming a tool or author is dropped, and an entry with an em dash is left out with a warning. `npm run typecheck` and `npm run check` create an empty file first (`--ensure`) so a fresh clone works. PRs no longer edit `lib/changelog.ts`. The footer and mobile menu link (`ChangelogLink`) shows a dot while the newest entry is newer than the date the viewer last opened the page (`localStorage` key `tcg_changelog_seen`). `scripts/check-changelog.mts`; `check-words` also scans the file for em dashes and old print wording.
- `/faq` is the single home for "how it works" content. The jump list and the body use the section order configured in the FAQ Editor (`/admin/faq`, backed by `site:faq`): default order is getting started, chat commands, cards & versions, trading, upgrading, your profile, showing off, **points & leaderboard**, seasons, extension, privacy, troubleshooting. Admins can reorder section blocks, hide sections, and add custom questions. Getting started says you earn packs by following, subscribing or gifting subs, and links to `#commands` instead of listing `!pack`. It also says paid pulls have no limit until staff set one for a role, staff can set a personal limit, and staff can add extra pulls for everyone for the current stream. Chat commands lists the enabled commands from `getCommands()`, then a fixed `!pack` line (set up in Mix It Up, not the command builder). Upgrading says any edition of any version can be traded, including an upgraded one, and that a favourite or an edition already in an offer cannot. The print-bonus sentence uses the live `serialBonusMax` and `serialBonusCutoff` ("the 40th print" only when that cutoff is 40); cutoff 0 keeps the switched-off sentence. It reads the current season, so upgrade costs, version odds and the per-card value table are always live (`app/(site)/faq/page.tsx` loads the data and sets the order; each section is a component in `app/(site)/faq/sections/<id>.tsx`, with `Section` and `Q` in `faq/parts.tsx`); link to anchors like `/faq#points`. The home page and the leaderboard only carry short pointers to it. If the rules change (costs, odds, scoring), check the FAQ wording too.

- Because the home page shows recent pulls publicly, the Privacy Policy says so; keep it in step if you expose more.

## Public top bar (`SiteNav`) and search
- **Phone tab bar** (`MobileTabBar`, rendered by `SiteNav`, below `xl`, the `<nav aria-label="Main">` there; the desktop top nav is the `Main` at `xl` and up and the bar is `xl:hidden`): fixed along the bottom, 56 px plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, with Home, Card Dex, Trade, Leaderboard and More (a signed-in viewer's second tab is instead **Collection**, `/u/<username>?tab=collection` with a book icon, active on every tab of their own profile and its sub-paths (the active rule compares the path only); Card Dex then moves into the More sheet, and `tabsFor(username)` in `MobileTabBar.tsx` is the one list both the bar and the sheet read, so no link is lost or doubled). Labels, hrefs and the active rule (`aria-current="page"`, yellow) come from the shared `LINKS` / `isActive` in `app/components/site/links.ts`, which the top nav and the menu also read. A tapped tab turns yellow and its icon pulses until the page arrives (`useLinkStatus` in `TabFace`), since most of those pages have no loading skeleton. **More** opens the **More sheet** (`BottomSheet.tsx`, a generic modal that slides up from the bottom; `role=dialog`, `aria-modal`, max 85dvh with its own scroll, safe-area padding, above the bar; Esc, a tap on the backdrop, a drag down past 80 px or a quick flick on its handle closes it; focus moves in, Tab stays inside, focus returns to More; no slide under `prefers-reduced-motion` or the Reduce motion setting). It holds the account row (avatar, name and rank chip linking to the profile, or Sign in with Twitch), the links the bar doesn't carry (Collectors, Season Stats, FAQ, and Card Dex when signed in), What's new, My stats / My profile / Settings, the staff panel link, Install app (`InstallButton row`) and Sign out. There is no burger drawer and below `xl` no account dropdown or top-bar login button (they show from `xl`, unchanged); the top bar is logo, search, the live badge (all widths) and bell (the install icon shows from `xl`). `SiteShell` pads the page by the bar's height below `xl` so the footer is never covered. The bar hides while a text field has focus (on-screen keyboard). Badges: Trade shows unread offers and counters, More shows all unread notifications; `NotificationBell` fires a `tcg:notice-counts` window event when its counts change and the bar listens, so the bar loads nothing itself (the badges need a signed-in viewer, the only one the bell renders for).
- **Dialogs and filters as sheets (below `md`):** the card detail dialogs (profile `CardDetail`, Card Dex `DexDetail`) and `ReportDialog` keep their markup and close behaviour but, below `md`, sit at the bottom: full width, square bottom corners, max 90dvh with their own scroll, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` padding, a slide-up (`.sheet-mobile` in `globals.css`, off under reduced motion) and a sticky grab handle (`SheetHandle` from `BottomSheet.tsx`, `md:hidden`; drag down to close). They are z-50 and above the tab bar (z-40). Inline filter / sort rows (Card Dex, the profile collection grid, the trade board toolbar) are wrapped in `FilterSheet`: from `md` they render as before, below `md` they sit behind a `Filters` button (a yellow dot while any filter is active) that opens them in a `BottomSheet` with a Done button; state stays in the parent. Before hydration the server renders both the button (`md:hidden`) and the inline block (`max-md:hidden`) so the page does not jump when a phone is detected (it caused the Card Dex layout shift). The cookie notice (`CookieNotice`) is server-rendered open so it paints with the page; a tiny inline script sets `data-cookie-ok` on `<html>` before paint for a visitor who already dismissed it, and `globals.css` hides `.cookie-notice` then. Not converted: site search, the keyboard-shortcuts help (desktop), the captcha challenge and the upgrade / trade ceremonies (full-screen by design).
- **Touch pass (below `md`, public site only):** `SiteShell`'s root carries `.site-touch`, which scopes the rules at the end of `globals.css` (the admin has none of them). **Tap targets**: every `button`, `summary`, `[role=button]`, `[role=tab]`, `a.btn` and `a[class*="rounded"]` (chips, pills) gets an invisible `::before` hit area that grows it to at least 44 x 44 px without changing its look (it adds nothing once the control is big enough, and sets `position: relative` unless the element is already positioned); add the `tap` class to any other small tappable element (an icon link such as the logo). Real tab links use `max-md:py-3`. Overlapping hit areas of neighbouring tiny controls: keep a few px between them. **Press feedback**: on touch (`hover: none`) or below `md`, a pressed button, summary, `a.btn` or rounded link darkens (`filter: brightness(.85)`, low specificity so the 3D yellow button's own `:active` wins); it never moves, so reduced motion needs nothing. **Inputs**: text inputs, selects and textareas are at least 16 px (iOS zooms on focus below that) and inputs / selects at least 44 px tall. **No hover-only controls**: nothing on the public site is revealed only by `:hover` (the `group-hover` uses are decorative; `InfoTip` opens on tap); keep it so, and give any new hover reveal a touch route (`@media (hover: none)` visible, or a button).
- **Phone-width rules (375 and 414 px sweep):** nothing scrolls sideways; anything sticky at the bottom of the page (the Settings save bar) sits above the tab bar below `xl` (`bottom-[calc(4.25rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]`), and a sticky bar under the header (the profile's selection bar) starts below the safe area. Scrolling tab strips (`TabStrip`: the profile and Trade tabs) open scrolled to the active tab. The stats "Every card" table drops the Collectors column below `sm` (shown under Pulls as "N owners") and lets card names wrap.
- **Search** (`SiteSearch`, mounted in `SiteShell`): press `/` or Ctrl/⌘+K (or the Search button; the phone menu has an entry) to find a collector, a card or a page. Pure logic in `lib/site-search.ts` (`cleanQuery`, `buildEntityItems`, `rankSearch` with a cap per kind; pages match only by name / word / keyword, never by scattered letters). Pages are matched in the browser; collectors and cards come from `GET /api/search?q=` (`lib/site-search-data.ts`, active season only, the collector list cached 30 s and the card list 60 s per server instance and sandbox). **Collectors an admin hid from public boards (`users:hidden`) are never returned.** Enter on a no-match opens `/collection/<typed>` directly. Cards link to `/cards?card=<number>`, which opens that card in the dex (`CardDex` `initialOpenId`). A new public page should be added to `PUBLIC_PAGES`.
- **Rank chip**: `SiteShell` looks the signed-in viewer up in the shared (20 s) board and passes `rank` to `SiteNav`; the chip shows `#N`, a ▲ / ▼ with the places gained or lost since they last opened the account menu (remembered in `localStorage` `tcg_rank_seen:<username>`, this browser only) and a tooltip with points. The chip is a nice-to-have: any failure just hides it.
- **Menus**: the account dropdown is a real menu (focus moves in, arrows / Home / End via `nextMenuIndex` in `lib/menu-nav.ts`, Esc returns focus to the button, Tab closes); the phone menu closes on Esc or a tap outside and returns focus. `scripts/check-site-search.mts` covers the search and menu logic.

## Public site foundations (SEO, sharing, errors, speed)
- **Titles**: the root layout's template adds ` · The Cheesy TCG`, so a page's `metadata.title` must NOT repeat the site name (`scripts/check-public-meta.mts` fails if one does). Every public page exports `metadata` / `generateMetadata` (the home page uses the default); dynamic ones: `/collection/[username]` ("alice's collection") and `/leaderboard/[seasonId]` (the season name). `/login` is the admin sign-in and is `noindex` via `app/login/layout.tsx`. Signed-in and utility pages (`/account`, `/trade`, `/trade/new`, `/auth/error`) are `noindex`.
- **Search lists** live in `lib/seo.ts`: `INDEXED_PATHS` (in the sitemap), `NOINDEX_PATHS`, `DISALLOWED_PATHS` (robots), and `buildSitemap()` (fixed pages + one leaderboard per published season). `app/sitemap.ts` / `app/robots.ts` / `app/manifest.ts` use them. **A new public page must be added to `INDEXED_PATHS` or `NOINDEX_PATHS`** or the check fails.
- **Share images** (next/og `ImageResponse`): `app/opengraph-image.tsx` (default) and `app/u/[username]/opengraph-image.tsx` (name, rank, points, progress for the active season). `lib/og.tsx` loads the brand icon from disk, falls back to fetching it, and degrades to no icon. In `ImageResponse` markup every `div` with more than one child needs `display: 'flex'` (interpolated text counts as several children, so use a template string).
- **Frames and states**: `app/not-found.tsx` (adds the site frame itself) vs `not-found.tsx` inside `(site)`, `collection` and `leaderboard` (no frame, because those layouts already provide it, otherwise the nav would appear twice); `NotFoundBody` is the shared content (with a find-a-collector box). `app/error.tsx` (no nav, since the nav may be what failed) and a `loading.tsx` skeleton (`SiteSkeleton`) only on pages that never link to themselves with different `?params`: home (`(site)/(home)/`, a route group so its skeleton covers only `/`), `collection` and `leaderboard/[seasonId]`. **Never add a `loading.tsx` to a page that changes its own search params** (profile `?tab=`, Card Dex, Season Stats, Trade, My stats `?season=`, Feedback `?kind=`), nor to a parent folder or route group above such a page: on Next 15 in production, once the param-less URL is prefetched, the same-page navigation reuses that prefetch, React never commits the new page, and the links look dead (it works in `next dev`). `(entity)` has none so `notFound()` answers a real 404. Instead, `PendingPage` (inside `SiteShell`'s `<main>`) swaps the page for `SiteSkeleton` the moment an internal link to another path is clicked (left click, no modifier, not `target=_blank`, `download` or `/api/`), scrolls to the top, and shows the page again when the path changes, or after 10 s if it never does. Same-path `?param` and `#hash` links keep the page in place, and Back / Forward show no skeleton. `SiteShell` has a "Skip to content" link and `<main id="main">`.
- **Leaderboard cache**: `getSeasonLeaderboardCached()` (`lib/leaderboard.ts`, built on the pure `createTtlCache` in `lib/ttl-cache.ts`) shares one board for ~20 s between visitors of the PUBLIC pages (home, stats, card dex via `getSeasonOverview`, the leaderboard page). The board also carries the collection it loaded, and `getCollectionCached(season)` returns it, so home, stats, the card dex and the profile read the `tcg_{collection}` blob at most once per 20 s per instance (`scripts/check-season-snapshot.mts`); a pull can take up to 20 s to show there. The trade page, account, upgrades and every write keep `getCollection`. It is per server instance, keyed by sandbox + season, shares in-flight work, never caches failures, and its result is read-only. Use `getSeasonLeaderboard` directly wherever a viewer has just changed their own cards (their collection, My Stats, upgrades, trades) and in the admin.

## Legal pages and FAQ checks

- `/privacy`, Privacy Policy and `/terms`, Terms of Use / EULA (public; the URLs to enter in the extension's dev.twitch.tv Privacy Policy / EULA fields). Content lives in `app/privacy/page.tsx` and `app/terms/page.tsx`; **keep them in step with what the app really collects** (Twitch IDs/usernames, collections, activity log, avatars, analytics, Discord/webhook forwarding). Bump `LEGAL_UPDATED` in `lib/legal.ts` when you change them.

- **FAQ wording is data.** Every built-in question lives in `lib/faq-builtins.ts` as `{ id, sectionId, question, answer, open?, when?, anchors? }`; the `id` is the `#anchor` (keep it, and add old anchors to `anchors`). Answers use the custom-answer syntax (paragraphs, `- ` lists, `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `` `code` ``, `[text](href)`). `{placeholder}` words are filled on render from `getFaqContext()` (`app/(site)/faq/context.ts`, also feeds the editor's reference list); an unknown `{word}` stays as typed. A line that is only `{{block}}` draws a live component from `app/(site)/faq/blocks.tsx` (`FAQ_BLOCK_NAMES` in `lib/faq-core.ts`). `when` shows a question only for `upgrades`, `no_upgrades` or `values`. Staff overrides (`overrides[id]` in `site:faq`: `question`, `answer`, `hidden`) replace the default text; text equal to the default is not stored. One renderer (`FaqAnswer` in `parts.tsx`) draws built-in and custom questions. Privacy and Terms are not editable.
- **FAQ upkeep, reinforced**: `scripts/check-words.mts` fails when a number quoted in the FAQ or Privacy Policy (retention caps, cookie lifetime, the Turnstile pass, saved alerts) no longer matches its constant, or the Cheesygidds-only rule goes missing from the FAQ, Terms or Privacy Policy; when you change one of those limits, update the wording in the same PR. `OpenOnHash` (`app/components/site/`) opens the FAQ question a `#hash` link points at.

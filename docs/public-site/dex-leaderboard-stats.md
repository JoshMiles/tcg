# Card dex, leaderboard, stats and achievements

> Read this when you touch the card dex, leaderboard, My Stats (profile Stats tab), achievements, copy pages and lookup 404s.

- **daisyUI on the dex and leaderboard:** dex filters use `input` and `select`. The dex dialog is a daisyUI `card` (the navy surface is part of `card`); its version table is `table table-sm`. Leaderboard rows are the same `card`, and the collector search is an `input`. The season-stats Every card search and table (`CardTable`) use `input` and `table` too. Rarity chips, the rank-by chips, cheese `.rind` sections, `holo-hover` and `vfx-*` on dex tiles stay. My Stats, account, settings and copy pages are later slices.

- **Leaderboard (`/leaderboard/<season>`)**: the list is the client `Board` (`app/leaderboard/Board.tsx`) over pure `lib/board-view.ts` (`rankBoard`, `filterBoard`, `rankMovement`): switch what it ranks by (Points / Collected / Pulls / Shinies; ties share a rank), search a collector (ranks never change when filtering, so you still see their true place), Complete only, 25 at a time with Show more, a row opens to its points breakdown (cards + completion bonus + complete set bonus), the signed-in viewer's row is highlighted with a **You are #N** jump button, and ▲▼ rank movement on the points board is remembered per season in `localStorage` `tcg_board_ranks:<seasonId>` (a fresh baseline is saved once the old one is an hour old). It still reads the shared cached board and shows `leaderboardSize` rows. **Season stats (`/stats`)** adds a public **Pulls per day** chart (`PullsChart`, server SVG; the `stats:h:` counters from `lib/stats.ts`, whole season at least a week, ended seasons stop at `endedAt`; it only counts from when tracking began and says so) and a sortable, searchable **Every card** table (`CardTable`: copies, collectors via `ownersByCard`, hidden / private collectors left out) in place of the most / least pulled lists. `scripts/check-board.mts` covers the ranking helpers.

- **Card Dex (`/cards`) tools**: the page sends only each card's collector count; the dialog's **Top collectors** (10, with avatars, hidden collectors left out) load when it opens from `GET /api/cards/<n>/owners?season=` (`ownersByCard` for one card, gated like `/api/search`), with a loading line meanwhile. Filtering and sorting are pure in `lib/dex-view.ts` (`applyDex`, `dexProgress`; `scripts/check-dex.mts`): search (name or #), rarity, pack (when 2+), Show (collected / missing for signed-in viewers of the active season, **Looking for**, **nobody has pulled it yet**), sort (number, rarest, most valuable, most pulled, most collectors, name, missing first) with a reverse button, a smaller-cards toggle (defaults to the viewer's Compact grids setting) and a progress bar. Tiles carry a purple search badge for wanted cards and an "unpulled" chip when nobody has the card. The card dialog adds **Add to Looking for** (`toggleWantedAction` in `app/(site)/cards/actions.ts`, the viewer's `UserSettings.wanted`, max 6, optimistic), **Share this card** (the canvas share image) under the art, and a **Browse every print** row below Advanced information (it links to `/cards/<n>/copies`).
- **My Stats extras (the owner's profile Stats tab, `OwnerStats` in `app/u/[username]/OwnerStats.tsx`; `/account` forwards there)**: **What to do next** (`lib/advisor.ts`, pure: best affordable upgrade with a positive projected gain, one-to-three cards from a complete collection, the missing card worth the most (its points + its share of the completion bonus, `missingGain`), points to pass the collector above), **Your score over time** (`lib/score-history.ts` + pure `score-history-core.ts`: `recordScoreSample` runs when the owner opens the tab in a live season, stores at most one sample per 30 minutes and only when score / rank changed or six hours passed, newest 120 kept in KV `user:<u>:scorehist:<seasonId>`; the SVG line needs two samples and honestly says it starts at the first visit), **Cards you still need** (the 8 most valuable missing cards with a Look for toggle that edits the profile wishlist through `toggleWantedAction`), **Share my stats** (canvas image of the top copies) and the earlier-collection notice (docs/public-site/accounts.md). The public breakdown, versions owned and most valuable editions are the same component every visitor gets (`StatsTab`), so the owner sees them once; other visitors see only that. Upgrades show only while the season is live. The profile Overview keeps just a short Score widget (points, rank, cards) linking to this tab. The old page's season picker (look back at an ended season), awards list and waiting-offers button are gone: awards sit in the profile header and offers on the Trades tab. `scripts/check-mystats.mts` covers the advisor and the sampling rules.
- **Achievements** (profile widget `achievements`, on by default; FAQ `#achievements`): collection goals per season from the pure `lib/achievements.ts` (`evaluateSeason`: **Completed <season>**, **All <rarity>** for each rarity that has cards, **All Reverse Holo / Full Art / Full Art Holo** for cards that can be pulled in that version, and the **Master set**; goals that make no sense for a season are left out). `lib/achievements-store.ts` `syncAchievements(username)` evaluates every published season the viewer has cards in, **remembers what is earned** in the KV hash `user:<u>:ach` (id `<seasonId>:<kind>` -> the achievement plus `earnedAt`, the first time it was seen earned) so trading cards away never takes one back, and returns `{ earned, next }` (`next` = unearned goals with some progress in a season that is still running). It runs when the widget is rendered (the owner renders every widget, so opening their own profile persists new ones). Staff switch a kind off, replace its title and detail, or configure an optional bonus reward from the buff catalogue, under Admin → Settings → Achievements (`achievements:settings`). A blank title or detail keeps the built-in words, which include the season name. A newly earned achievement awards its bonus once (stored achievements are never re-awarded on later syncs, and switching a kind off later does not strip a bonus already granted). `evaluateSeason` leaves a switched-off kind out; a goal already stored on a profile stays, and clearing the override shows those stored words again. Awards (season leaderboard prizes from the wrap-up snapshot) are a different thing. `scripts/check-achievements.mts` (mock KV) covers the goals, progress, sorting and persistence.
- **Serial milestones**: `serialGoals()` in `lib/achievements.ts` adds four goals per season when the season's copies are numbered (`AchSeason.held` = the viewer's edition numbers by entry, from `loadSerialIndex`; `bonusCutoff` from the scoring settings): **First edition** (hold edition #1 of any card), **First shiny edition** (a #1 Full Art Holo; only in seasons with shinies), **Five firsts** (#1 of five different cards, capped by the card count) and **Early bird** (a copy inside the serial bonus cutoff; absent when the cutoff is 0). Like every achievement they are remembered when earned and never taken back. **First-ever pull moment**: when a real pull is edition #1 the overlay (`firstEverMoment()` in `widget.js`, props `reveal.firstEver` / `firstEverText` / `firstEverHold`, section "First-ever moment") adds a gold FIRST EVER banner with the version, two gold sparkle bursts and keeps the card up longer (`extendHold`, the hide timer is extendable until it fires); not for chat show-offs. Test panel: **First-ever pull [1]** (dry run, sample serial 1). A #1 pull is also "notable" everywhere: `notableReason` (home Recent pulls chip "First ever", the admin activity feed "First ever pull", alerts) and the Discord pull embed starts with FIRST EVER.
- **Achievement Editor (ROADMAP 33.2)**: the default title and detail of every built-in kind are templates in `DEFAULT_TEXT` (`lib/achievements.ts`, `defaultText(kind)`); `evaluateSeason` / `serialGoals` build their sentences from them, and `applyAchievementText` fills a saved override the same way on the way out (stored rows keep working). Placeholders: `{season}` (empty for trading goals), `{need}`, `{rarity}`, `{version}`, `{cutoff}` (Early bird, `Achievement.cutoff`); custom goals also take `{n}`; an unknown `{word}` is shown as typed. Override text equal to the default is not stored. **Custom goals** (KV `achievements:custom`, `normalizeCustomGoals`): rules `unique`, `editions`, `rarity`, `version`, `firsts`, `trades`, per season from `AchSeason` (`evaluateSeason(s, disabled, custom)`), id `<seasonId>:custom-<id>`, N capped at the qualifying cards (not for `editions`), no qualifying cards means no goal. **Trade goals** (`first-trade`, `ten-trades`, custom `trades`) live under the pseudo-season `trading` (`tradingGoals(count, ...)`, count from `completedTradeCount` in `lib/trades.ts`: accepted trades on either side, all seasons, one history read per sync); their progress shows in `next` too. **Stats**: when `syncAchievements` stores a newly earned goal and wins the claim marker `user:<u>:ach:counted:<id>` it does `hincrby achievements:earned <kind>` and pushes to `achievements:recent` (trimmed to 25); `recountAchievements()` rebuilds the counts from every user's `user:<u>:ach` hash. A goal's buff goes through the same paid-marker path as built-ins (custom goals carry their own `buffId`).

## Public routes
- `/`, home (see above); `/stats`, `/cards`

- **Copy page wording**: a copy is never "made for" someone. Its first journey step and the date tile read by origin (`ORIGIN_VERB`, `madeStep` in `ProvenanceTimeline`): Pulled by, Given to, Forged by (an upgrade), or "Already held by ... when copies were first numbered". The unique copy number is a small `text-xl` line under the title, not the headline.
- **Lookup pages answer a real 404 (`app/(entity)/`)**: `/copy/<season>/<copy>`, `/c/<uid>` (a 307 redirect when found) and `/cards/<card>/copies` live in their own route group with the same `SiteShell` frame as `(site)` but **no `loading.tsx`**. A loading skeleton makes Next.js send the page shell (HTTP 200) before the page runs, so `notFound()` could only show "That page isn't here" with a 200 (plus `noindex`). Keep new look-up-by-address pages in `(entity)`. The dex dialog, when opened by a `?card=` link, drops that parameter on close (so a refresh or Back does not reopen it).
- **An ended season's board is its saved final standings**: when a season has `endedAt` and a wrap-up snapshot, `/leaderboard/<season>` lists the snapshot's standings (`standingsToBoardRows` in `lib/board-view.ts`; hidden / private collectors filtered at read time), so the list always agrees with the champion and awards above it (the live numbers drift once serials are numbered later or scoring changes). `SnapshotRow` now also keeps `completionBonus`, `setBonus` and `variants`; a snapshot taken before that shows the bonuses as one "Bonuses" lump, no RH / FA / SH chips and no Shinies ranking. No snapshot: the live board. `app/leaderboard/[seasonId]/layout.tsx` checks the season exists above the page's `loading.tsx`, so an unknown season is a real 404 while the board still shows its skeleton (the index page redirects and has none).

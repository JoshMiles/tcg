# Card dex, leaderboard, stats and achievements

> Read this when you touch the card dex, leaderboard, My Stats, achievements, copy pages and lookup 404s.

- **Leaderboard (`/leaderboard/<season>`)**: the list is the client `Board` (`app/leaderboard/Board.tsx`) over pure `lib/board-view.ts` (`rankBoard`, `filterBoard`, `rankMovement`): switch what it ranks by (Points / Different cards / Copies / Shinies; ties share a rank), search a collector (ranks never change when filtering, so you still see their true place), Complete only, 25 at a time with Show more, a row opens to its points breakdown (cards + completion bonus + complete set bonus), the signed-in viewer's row is highlighted with a **You are #N** jump button, and ▲▼ rank movement on the points board is remembered per season in `localStorage` `tcg_board_ranks:<seasonId>` (a fresh baseline is saved once the old one is an hour old). It still reads the shared cached board and shows `leaderboardSize` rows. **Season stats (`/stats`)** adds a public **Pulls per day** chart (`PullsChart`, server SVG; the `stats:h:` counters from `lib/stats.ts`, whole season at least a week, ended seasons stop at `endedAt`; it only counts from when tracking began and says so) and a sortable, searchable **Every card** table (`CardTable`: copies, collectors via `ownersByCard`, hidden / private collectors left out) in place of the most / least pulled lists. `scripts/check-board.mts` covers the ranking helpers.

- **Card Dex (`/cards`) tools**: filtering and sorting are pure in `lib/dex-view.ts` (`applyDex`, `dexProgress`; `scripts/check-dex.mts`): search (name or #), rarity, pack (when 2+), Show (collected / missing for signed-in viewers of the active season, **Looking for**, **nobody has pulled it yet**), sort (number, rarest, most valuable, most pulled, most collectors, name, missing first) with a reverse button, a smaller-cards toggle (defaults to the viewer's Compact grids setting) and a progress bar. Tiles carry a purple search badge for wanted cards and an "unpulled" chip when nobody has the card. The card dialog adds **Add to Looking for** (`toggleWantedAction` in `app/(site)/cards/actions.ts`, the viewer's `UserSettings.wanted`, max 6, optimistic), **Share this card** (the canvas share image) under the art, and a **Browse every print** row below Advanced information (it links to `/cards/<n>/copies`).
- **My Stats (`/account`) extras**: **What to do next** (`lib/advisor.ts`, pure: best affordable upgrade with a positive projected gain, one-to-three cards from a complete collection, the missing card worth the most (its points + its share of the completion bonus, `missingGain`), points to pass the collector above), **Your score over time** (`lib/score-history.ts` + pure `score-history-core.ts`: `recordScoreSample` runs when the viewer opens the page for the active season, stores at most one sample per 30 minutes and only when score / rank changed or six hours passed, newest 120 kept in KV `user:<u>:scorehist:<seasonId>`; the SVG line needs two samples and honestly says it starts at the first visit), **Cards you still need** (the 8 most valuable missing cards with a Look for toggle that edits the profile wishlist through `toggleWantedAction`), awards earned, a waiting-offers button, **Share my stats** (canvas image of the top copies) and a season picker (`?season=`; an ended / earlier season is a read-only look back with no upgrades or history). `scripts/check-mystats.mts` covers the advisor and the sampling rules.
- **Achievements** (profile widget `achievements`, on by default; FAQ `#achievements`): collection goals per season from the pure `lib/achievements.ts` (`evaluateSeason`: **Completed <season>**, **All <rarity>** for each rarity that has cards, **All Reverse Holo / Full Art / Shiny** for cards that can be pulled in that version, and the **Master set**; goals that make no sense for a season are left out). `lib/achievements-store.ts` `syncAchievements(username)` evaluates every published season the viewer has cards in, **remembers what is earned** in the KV hash `user:<u>:ach` (id `<seasonId>:<kind>` -> the achievement plus `earnedAt`, the first time it was seen earned) so trading cards away never takes one back, and returns `{ earned, next }` (`next` = unearned goals with some progress in a season that is still running). It runs when the widget is rendered (the owner renders every widget, so opening their own profile persists new ones). Awards (season leaderboard prizes from the wrap-up snapshot) are a different thing. `scripts/check-achievements.mts` (mock KV) covers the goals, progress, sorting and persistence.
- **Serial milestones**: `serialGoals()` in `lib/achievements.ts` adds four goals per season when the season's copies are numbered (`AchSeason.held` = the viewer's edition numbers by entry, from `loadSerialIndex`; `bonusCutoff` from the scoring settings): **First edition** (hold edition #1 of any card), **First shiny edition** (a #1 Shiny Full Art; only in seasons with shinies), **Five firsts** (#1 of five different cards, capped by the card count) and **Early bird** (a copy inside the serial bonus cutoff; absent when the cutoff is 0). Like every achievement they are remembered when earned and never taken back. **First-ever pull moment**: when a real pull is edition #1 the overlay (`firstEverMoment()` in `widget.js`, props `reveal.firstEver` / `firstEverText` / `firstEverHold`, section "First-ever moment") adds a gold FIRST EVER banner with the version, two gold sparkle bursts and keeps the card up longer (`extendHold`, the hide timer is extendable until it fires); not for chat show-offs. Test panel: **First-ever pull [1]** (dry run, sample serial 1). A #1 pull is also "notable" everywhere: `notableReason` (home Recent pulls chip "First ever", the admin activity feed "First ever pull", alerts) and the Discord pull embed starts with FIRST EVER.

## Public routes
- `/`, home (see above); `/stats`, `/cards`

- **Copy page wording**: a copy is never "made for" someone. Its first journey step and the date tile read by origin (`ORIGIN_VERB`, `madeStep` in `ProvenanceTimeline`): Pulled by, Given to, Forged by (an upgrade), or "Already held by ... when copies were first numbered". The unique copy number is a small `text-xl` line under the title, not the headline.
- **Lookup pages answer a real 404 (`app/(entity)/`)**: `/copy/<season>/<copy>`, `/c/<uid>` (a 307 redirect when found) and `/cards/<card>/copies` live in their own route group with the same `SiteShell` frame as `(site)` but **no `loading.tsx`**. A loading skeleton makes Next.js send the page shell (HTTP 200) before the page runs, so `notFound()` could only show "That page isn't here" with a 200 (plus `noindex`). Keep new look-up-by-address pages in `(entity)`. The dex dialog, when opened by a `?card=` link, drops that parameter on close (so a refresh or Back does not reopen it).
- **An ended season's board is its saved final standings**: when a season has `endedAt` and a wrap-up snapshot, `/leaderboard/<season>` lists the snapshot's standings (`standingsToBoardRows` in `lib/board-view.ts`; hidden / private collectors filtered at read time), so the list always agrees with the champion and awards above it (the live numbers drift once serials are numbered later or scoring changes). `SnapshotRow` now also keeps `completionBonus`, `setBonus` and `variants`; a snapshot taken before that shows the bonuses as one "Bonuses" lump, no RH / FA / SH chips and no Shinies ranking. No snapshot: the live board. `app/leaderboard/[seasonId]/layout.tsx` checks the season exists above the page's `loading.tsx`, so an unknown season is a real 404 while the board still shows its skeleton (the index page redirects and has none).

# Card serials (unique copies)

> Read this when you touch the copy ledger, serial numbers, unique copy numbers, copy pages, flip cards or share images of copies.

## Card serials (unique copies)
**Wording (ROADMAP 25.2, decided 2026-10-20):** in UI text a copy is an **edition** ("your recent editions"). Its number within one card + version is the **edition number** (`Edition 7 of 12`, `Edition #7` when the total is unknown; `Copy.n`), and the points bonus on that number is the **edition bonus** (the code name `serialMultiplier` stays). Its game-wide unique id is the **serial number** (`Serial #1023381`, `Copy.uid`). The word "print" is not used in UI text for a copy or its number. "Copy", "serial", "print" and `uid` in the rest of this doc are the code-level names (types, KV keys, URLs such as `/copy/...`, `/c/<uid>` and its alias `/p/<uid>`). Card-back tokens `{printNumber}` and `{edition}` keep their names: `{printNumber}` renders the serial number and `{edition}` renders the "Edition n of N" line. `scripts/check-words.mts` bans "unique copy number", "unique number", "copy page", "ID #", "print bonus", "Print #", and "Edition #" in front of a serial number. **Counts (ROADMAP 37.1):** one word per idea. **Collected** is how many different cards of a set a player owns (`23/25 collected`); **pulls** is every card pulled, repeats included (`194 pulls`, the leaderboard's big number label too); **edition** is only ever one numbered copy, never a count or total. Repeats keep the ×N badge. The Twitch panel still uses the old words until the next extension version.
Every copy of a card has an identity: `<card>.<variant index>.<n>` (e.g. `7.3.12`), where **n counts up per card AND version** (the first Full Art Holo of a card is Full Art Holo #1, independent of its Regulars). Collections are unchanged (`number[]` per viewer, source of truth for scores etc.); a **ledger** sits beside them.
- **Pure** `lib/serials-core.ts` (ids, `diffCounts`, `newestFirst`, history events, labels) and **server** `lib/serials.ts` (KV per collection name: `copies:<c>:o:<owner>` hash of Copy records, `copies:<c>:idx` id → owner, `copies:<c>:n` entry → ever made = next serial, `copies:<c>:ret` retired copies with history, `copies:<c>:init` flag). An owner is a viewer or `escrow:<tradeId>`.
- **One choke point**: `saveCollection(name, data, ctx?)` in `lib/kv.ts` reads the previous blob (only when tracking) and calls `syncAfterSave`, which mints what was added, retires what was removed (newest serial first, history kept) and never throws. `ctx.origin` says where new copies came from (`addCollectionEntry(..., origin)`: `'pull'` for the overlay, `'grant'` for admin / webhook grants); `ctx.moves` makes trades keep identity (offer → escrow, escrow → taker, taker → offerer, refund → offerer; the **newest** copies move first); `ctx.upgrade` retires the spent copies (`upgraded`) and mints the new one with `madeFrom` pointing back. `ctx.restore` puts exact print ids back (revive retired ones, retire the rest of that viewer's prints) and `ctx.restoreRetired` revives prints that viewer had removed before the count reconcile, so a restore does not mint a second set. A refund of a numbered offer moves only the prints still held by `escrow:<tradeId>`. New code that changes collections just calls `saveCollection`; pass `moves` only if copies must keep their identity.
- **Tracking is off per collection until `backfillSerials`** (Admin → Settings → Twitch & viewers → Card editions → "Number existing editions", under the collection lock, safe to repeat). Existing copies get estimated numbers (`est`, flagged "~" / "estimated number"); everything after is exact. Sandboxes don't copy the ledger, so a sandbox needs its own backfill.
- **Surfaces**: serial chips in the profile card detail (`GridCard.copies`), the public copy page `/copy/<seasonId>/<copyId>` (card, "#n of N made", holder, origin, full history; viewers an admin hid or who made their profile private are shown as "a private collector"; noindex), and **share images** (`lib/share-image.ts` layout, `app/components/ShareImage.tsx` draws a 1080×1350 PNG on a canvas in the browser and shares or downloads it): "Share my cards" on a profile (best 6 cards with version and lowest serial) and "Share this card" on a copy page. History keeps the first event plus the latest 40. **The copy page's share image** has an engraved steel **plate** under the card (`ShareSpec.plate`, `SharePlate` in `lib/share-image.ts`; drawn by `drawPlate` in `ShareImage.tsx`): the unique copy number (gold when it earns a bonus), the edition line, the pulled / numbered date, who holds it (masked for private collectors) and a QR code to the copy (`lib/qr.ts`, shared with the flip card); the footer is the short link `/c/<number>`. The card shrinks to leave room (`cardAreaWithPlate` / `plateRect`; layout checked in `check-serials.mts`). Other share images (profile, stats, Card Dex) have no plate.
- **Browsing serials**: copies of a card + version are numbered 1..made with none skipped, so a page of them is just a range of ids (`pageCopies(c, card, variant, page, size)` in `lib/serials.ts`, live and retired together; `liveCounts` tallies live copies per entry). **Admin → Serials** (`/admin/serials`, nav key `n`, a GET-filtered server page: season, card, version, holder via `UserInput`; the overview grid shows made (held) per card and version, clicking a cell lists its copies newest first, a holder lists everything they hold) and **`/admin/serials/[seasonId]/[copyId]`** (unmasked details, `madeFrom` links, full history, and two actions in `app/admin/serial-actions.ts`: **Give to another viewer** and **Remove from the holder's collection**, both under the collection lock, both refused while the copy is in a trade offer, both leaving the collection array and the ledger in step). To act on one exact copy, `saveCollection`'s `SyncContext` takes `moves[].ids` / `note` (move those copies instead of the newest `count`) and `retire: { owner, ids, who, note }` (retire exactly those). Public: the Card Dex dialog's **Browse every edition** link opens the page `/cards/[card]/copies?season=&variant=&page=` (server-rendered from `pageCopies`: a tab per version, 25 per page newest first; holders an admin hid or who went private show as a private collector, a copy in an offer as "in a trade offer", a retired one with how it ended), and each row links to the public copy page. The dialog's **Share this card** button sits under the art, below "Showing: <version>".
- **Unique copy numbers (`Copy.uid`)**: on top of the edition number `n` (per card + version, drives the points bonus) every copy has a number that is unique across the whole game, e.g. `#1023381`. Handed out by a global counter (KV hash `copies:uid:seq`, `UID_BASE` = 1,000,000 so they are all 7 digits) inside `mint()`, kept by the copy through trades (it is the same record), new for an upgrade's result, retained after retirement. `copies:uid:idx` maps uid -> `<collection>|<copy id>` (`findByUid()`), so `/c/<uid>` redirects to `/copy/<season>/<copyId>` for any season, and the site search recognises a typed `#1023381` (`parseUid()`, 7-9 digits only, a `copy` result kind). **Existing copies**: `backfillSerials` numbers new ledger entries without a uid and then `assignMissingUids(c)` gives every copy that has none (live and retired) a number, oldest first (`at`, then card, version, edition); the Settings -> Twitch & viewers -> Card editions panel has **Give serial numbers** for collections that are already tracked (safe to repeat). Counters are per sandbox like all keys. Shown: copy page (big Serial #uid, with "Edition n of N" under it), the engraved flip-card back (the uid is the engraved serial number, the edition line under it), the card dialog (Edition #n and Serial #uid), admin copy page. The overlay pill and activity feed still show the print (`#7`) unless the format is set to the serial number.
- **Showcase copy (profile widget `pinned`)**: the owner pins one copy of theirs (`UserSettings.pinned` = its unique number; sanitised to a plausible uid, **checked against the live owner every time it is shown**, so a copy traded away simply stops being shown). Pinned from the copy page's **Pin to my profile** button (`PinCopyButton`, only for the holder; `pinCopyAction` in `app/(entity)/copy/actions.ts`: viewer from the session, the copy must be theirs, and the widget is added to their layout via `withPinnedWidget`) and unpinned from the same button or the widget. The widget shows the copy as a **FlipCard** (engraved unique number on the back, QR, journey link), its number and edition, and a **Share this copy** button. `loadCopyByUid` (`lib/copy-view.ts`) finds the copy + season + card from a unique number (any season); the copy's share image spec is built in one place, `copyShareSpec` (`lib/copy-share.ts`), used by the copy page and the widget. Checks in `scripts/check-user-settings.mts`.
- **Journey (copy page)**: the plain history list is now a provenance timeline. Pure `lib/provenance.ts` (`buildProvenance(copy, now)` -> steps made / offered / passed / returned / forged / removed with who held it after each step and for how long (time in a trade offer is nobody's), the holder list, completed trades, the longest hold, age (stops when the copy is spent) and state; `spanText`; `scripts/check-provenance.mts`) and the server component `app/components/ProvenanceTimeline.tsx` (summary tiles, a stacked "who held it" bar, the forged-from / forged-into links by unique number, the step list). Names are masked by the page (`who` / `plain`), times shown in UTC, and the copy keeps only its first event plus the latest 40, so the middle of a very busy copy is missing (the page says so).
- **Flip card** (`app/components/FlipCard.tsx`, client; pure back data in `lib/flip-back.ts`: `FlipBack`, `engravedDate` (UTC, so server and browser print the same), `madeLine`; CSS `.flip-*` in `globals.css`): the front is whatever is passed as children (the normal art tile with its holo), the back is an engraved light-steel plate with the serial (gold when it earns a bonus), "of N made", version, the date, origin and holder, and (copy page only) the last 3 history lines plus a QR code (`qrcode-generator`, drawn as one SVG path) and (card dialog) a "Full history" link. Pointer tilt (±12°, glare follows it; `touch-action: pan-y` so scrolling still works), tap / the real **Flip** button turns it over (the button is the keyboard route; the card itself is not a button because the back holds a link). Reduced motion (device setting or `data-motion="reduce"`) swaps the turn and tilt for a fade. Used on `/copy/<season>/<copy>` and in the profile card dialog (back = the lowest-numbered copy of the version shown; `GridCard.copies` carries `at`, `origin`, `made` for it). Holders are masked the same way as on the copy page. `scripts/check-flip-back.mts` covers the date and wording.
- `scripts/check-serials.mts` (mock KV) covers ids, backfill, per-version numbering, removals, trade identity, refunds, upgrades, layout and production isolation.
- **Serials are shown prominently everywhere** (`SerialChip`, a daisyUI `badge` in `app/components/SerialChip.tsx`: `#7`, gold with the multiplier when it earns a bonus, a gradient "first ever" for #1): on the **overlay reveal** (a pill top-left of the card opposite the label, `showSerial()` in `widget.js`, property `reveal.showSerial`; the number comes from `POST /api/collection` which now returns `{ serial, serialMult }` via `addCollectionEntryWithSerial` / `lastSerial`, read under the collection lock so it is the copy just made; dry runs and the editor still show a sample), in the **pull activity events** (`serial` + `bonus` on the `pull` event: the home Recent pulls, the admin feed and event detail, the plain-text line, the Discord embed's Serial field), on the **collection tiles** (lowest serial of the version shown) and in the card dialog's **prints tiles** (one tile per print with its edition chip and `Serial #uid`; see "Editions-first card dialog" in docs/public-site.md; `GridCard.copies[].m` / `.p` / `.j` come from `buildGridCards`), on the **copy page** (big number, "Worth N pts instead of M" and the bonus), in the **upgrade ceremony** (`upgradeCard` returns the new copy's `serial` / `serialMult`; web and panel), on the **trade stacks** (`TradeStacks` `pool`: the serials that would move, newest first) and as a mark on the **Twitch panel tiles**.
- **Profile Recent pulls (ROADMAP 2.3, widget id `recent`)**: the owner's latest 8 prints whose origin is `pull` or `grant` (webhook rewards and admin gifts, labelled "Granted" while still held) and whose first event names them (`pickRecentPulls` in `lib/recent-pulls-core.ts`), whether still held, in an offer, traded away, upgraded or removed (`pullFate`). Candidates are the prints they hold plus the ones their `pull` and `grant` activity events name (`copyId`, or derived from the edition), read with `getCopy` (live or retired). Each row: card + version, `Edition #n` + what became of it, edition chip, age, opening the print. Untracked collection: the owner sees their pull events (no numbers), visitors see nothing. A hidden profile only shows prints it still holds (its events are not read).

- **Events point at the print (ROADMAP 2.1, 2.2)**: `pull`, `grant` and `upgrade` activity events carry `copyId` / `uid` (older pulls only have `serial`, so the id is derived). `copyHrefFor` / `adminCopyHrefFor` in `lib/copy-link.ts` give the link: the home Recent pulls (`PullItem.href` / `.uid`, "Edition #..." under the edition chip) and the admin and mod activity feeds (card name, plus an "Open this print" button in the event detail; admin goes to `/admin/serials/...`, mods to the public `/c/<uid>`) use it. The feed gets a collection -> season id map (`PrintSeasons`) because events only know their collection.

- **Edition of the week (ROADMAP 6.2)**: a print with an interesting journey, featured on the home page. Pure `lib/print-of-week.ts`: `scorePrint(copy, now)` (distinct holders x3, completed trades x2 from `buildProvenance`, edition #1 +5, Full Art Holo / Full Art +2, age in weeks capped at 8, forged +2; null for a spent print, one in a trade offer or one without a serial number), `pickPrintOfWeek(candidates, recentUids)` (highest score, the older print on a tie, nothing picked in the last 8 weeks), `isoWeekKey`. Server `lib/print-of-week-store.ts`: KV `potw:current` (`PotwPick`: uid, seasonId, copyId, score, reason, chosenAt, `by` = 'auto' or the staff login), `potw:history` (newest 20 `{uid, at}`), `potw:week:<yyyy-Www>` (NX lock, 21 days). `scanSeason` reads every viewer in the active season's collection blob and their ledger hash (about 0.5 s for 15,000 prints / 313 viewers on the mock KV; real KV adds a round trip per batch of 10 viewers). `pickOncePerWeek` is the weekly cron's step (docs/hosting.md); `pickNow` is the admin **Pick again now** (Admin -> Prints, with **Clear**); **Feature as edition of the week** sits on `/admin/serials/<season>/<copy>`. All admin actions are in `app/admin/serial-actions.ts` and write an audit entry for the holder. Home: `PrintOfWeek` is titled "Card of the week" and shows the flip card, the card name, Serial #uid, the edition line (`editionLine`), Held by (masked like the edition page), See its journey, then the reason in smaller type; `loadFeatured` returns null when there is no pick or the print was spent (owner gone) or renumbered, so the section just disappears. `scripts/check-print-of-week.mts` covers scoring, the skip rules, the cron (secret, production only, sandbox, once a week) and the store.
- **Profile Rarest prints and Showcase (ROADMAP 8.1, 8.2)**: the `rarest` widget lists the holder's 5 rarest prints (`rarestPrints` in `lib/collection-view.ts`: one per card + version, their lowest edition of it, ordered by the chance of that card in that version = `pullRate` x the version's share of `maths.weights`, ties to the lower edition), each opening `/c/<uid>`; an untracked season keeps the card-level list. The `showcase` tiles open the holder's best print (`bestPrintOf`) of the card + version that earned the place.

- **Admin Editions screen (ROADMAP 10.5)**: `/admin/serials` is one list driven by the URL (`?season=&card=&v=&holder=&origin=&q=&page=&print=`, so a view can be shared between staff). Top: tiles (made, held, in offers via `inOfferCount`, retired, last numbered via `lastUid`, numbering status) and one search box (`parsePrintSearch` in `lib/serials-view.ts`: `#1023381` / `1023381` jumps to that print through `findByUid`, an exact viewer login lists their prints, otherwise a card name or number narrows the list). Main: a card list (art, rarity, made (held) per version); opening a card shows its editions as chips (edition, edition number, holder, a star when the edition earns a bonus), oldest first, with version tabs, a holder filter (a login, `offer` or `retired`) and an origin filter (`filterEditions`; with a filter the first 600 editions are read, otherwise 120 per page). A chip opens `?print=<copyId>`: a side panel on desktop, a full-screen sheet on phones, closed with a plain link so the view keeps its filters. The panel is `PrintDetail` (`app/admin/serials/PrintDetail.tsx`: details, Give, Feature as edition of the week, Remove, history), the same component the deep-link page `/admin/serials/<season>/<copy>` uses. The panel's forms carry a hidden `ret` (checked by `safeSerialsReturn`: only `/admin/serials?...`), so the actions in `serial-actions.ts` come back to the list with the panel open; without it they come back to the print's page. Check: `scripts/check-serials-view.mts`.

## The card back model (v2, ROADMAP 20.3a)

A pack's back design is `CardBackStyle` v2 in `lib/card-back.ts` (stored in `pack:<id>:cardback`): a background, pattern and edge, plus a list of **elements** (serial number, edition, name, date, origin, history, QR, logo, site name, link, up to 8 text boxes), each shown or hidden and placed in the **stack** (today's centred column, in list order) or **free** (a box in percent of the card). `sanitizeCardBack` accepts v1 (the 20.1 shape) and v2 and always returns v2; `upgradeCardBack` turns a v1 design into stack elements that render exactly as before, and `toV1` is the 20.1 editor's view of a design (kept in `lib/card-back-v1.ts` until 20.3b replaces it). `layoutBack` (`lib/card-back-layout.ts`, pure) turns a design and a print into nodes; `FlipCard` draws them (stack nodes keep the classic classes and CSS variables, so the stylesheet draws them as it always did; free nodes carry pixel positions and effects). Curated OFL fonts live in `public/fonts/back/` with their licences and load only when a back uses them (`lib/card-back-fonts.ts`). A stacked text element keeps the stylesheet's face until its font is not that face (site display for the serial number, site body for every other line); `layoutBack` then sets the number through `--num-font` and the other lines through `font-family`. `scripts/check-card-back.mts` checks the upgrade against the v1 variables and a node-list snapshot (`UPDATE_SNAPSHOT=1` refreshes it on purpose).

## The back everywhere (ROADMAP 20.3c)

- **One constructor:** `flipBackFor(print, style)` in `lib/flip-back.ts` is the only way to build a `FlipBack` (`sampleFlipBack` is its sample for editors and the workspace preview). `scripts/check-card-back.mts` fails when a file under `app/` or `lib/` builds one by hand.
- **Where a back shows:** copy page, card dialog, binder / profile widgets (pinned, showcase), edition of the week, the pack workspace preview, the admin Prints side panel and `/admin/serials/<season>/<copy>` (`PrintDetail`, flip card with the real holder), the copy share image, and the overlay. Trade pages show stacks of cards, not prints, and no OG image draws a back, so neither has one. The Twitch panel is frozen and keeps its own.
- **Share image:** `lib/card-back-canvas.ts` (`placeBack`: the layout engine's nodes as canvas boxes; stack nodes flowed down a centred column at a 240 px reference width, free nodes where the engine put them; effect strings parsed) and `drawBackCard` in `ShareImage.tsx`. A copy whose pack has a designed back is drawn as the card and its back side by side (`SharePlate.back`); fonts are awaited with `loadBackFonts` (`document.fonts.load`). The background pattern is drawn too (`canvasPattern` in `lib/card-back-canvas.ts`: the classic lines and holes by the canvas itself, other patterns as the same SVG the DOM uses, drawn over the plate with the design's blend mode). Not drawn: line wrapping of stack text (squeezed instead). A pack with no design keeps the slim plate.
- **Overlay:** `/api/overlay/config` returns `backs[packId]` (`lib/card-back-overlay.ts`: the nodes laid out at 240 x 360 with the facts about a print hidden, the plate's CSS variables and the font files). `public/widget.js` (`backNodeSpec` / `mountDesignBack`, between the BACK-RENDERER markers) draws them as DOM under `.tcg-back` (rules in `widget.css`, copied from `.flip-*`) and scales the plate to the card. Overlay Editor property `reveal.flipBack` (`cover` default, `design`) covers the flip reveal and the show-off flip. A rarity-coloured element shows grey there (the print's rarity is not known when the pack's back is prepared). The check runs the widget's own renderer against the server's node list.
### The card back editor (20.3b)

`app/admin/components/CardBackEditor.tsx` keeps its props and server actions: in the pack panel it shows the live back, presets, Copy from another pack, Reset and Save, and **Edit design** opens the visual editor (`card-back/Canvas.tsx`, `Layers.tsx`, `Properties.tsx`, whose Character and Effects controls live in the shared `workspace/CharacterPanel.tsx` (also used by the Overlay Editor); pure operations in `lib/card-back-edit.ts`). The canvas draws the real back through `FlipCard bare` and lays one box per element over it, measured from the DOM (`data-back-id`): drag to move (an element still in the stack is taken out of it, keeping its place and type size at the print page's 260 px), the right handle sets the width, Shift-drag the corner scales the text, the round handle rotates; snap lines come from the card and the other boxes plus a 2 % grid (Alt = free); arrows nudge. A whole drag is one undo step. The editor saves through `saveCardBackAction` like the 20.1 one did. Every text element, stacked or free, has the same font list (`BACK_FONTS`: site display, site body, system mono, and the self-hosted faces). A stacked element keeps today's face until someone picks one. **Font library**: `BACK_FONTS` has 44 faces (3 site / system ones and 41 OFL faces self-hosted in `public/fonts/back/`, a `<name>.LICENSE.txt` beside each; latin subsets, sourced from the Fontsource packages, nothing loads from a font service). Each `BackFont` lists the `weights` it really has (the Weight select offers only those, named Thin to Black via `WEIGHT_NAMES`); a variable font is one `file` (a weight range in the `FontFace`), a static one has a file per weight in `files`; `italic` + `italicFile` mark a family with a true italic (Nunito, Playfair Display, Inter, Merriweather, Lora), loaded when an element is italic, otherwise the browser slants the upright face. A saved weight a font lacks snaps to the nearest (`nearestWeight`). `loadBackFont(id, weight, italic)` serves the DOM, the overlay (`overlayBack().fonts`, `loadFace` in widget.js) and the share image (`loadBackFonts`, which waits before the canvas draws). `scripts/check-card-back.mts` checks every file and licence exists and an unknown id falls back. The overlay's text layers still use each font's 700 cut (an overlay weight control is ROADMAP 38.8). The editors pick a font with the shared `FontPicker` (`app/admin/components/workspace/`, props only: fonts, value, sample text, callbacks): each row in its own face with the text's own words (the font name when empty), a search box, category tabs (`category` on each `BackFont`: Display, Sans, Serif, Script, Fantasy, Mono / Pixel, Site), Recently used (last 5) and starred Favourites at the top (localStorage `tcg:font-recent` and `tcg:font-fav`, shared by both editors), a font file fetched only when its row scrolls into view, and a hover (or arrow-key) preview: the card back editor shows it through a transient `preview` style that never enters the design or undo. To add a font: copy the latin woff2 and licence from its Fontsource package, add a `BACK_FONT_DEFS` entry.

**Text style model and sizes.** The type and colour fields of a text (`size`, font, weight, italic, underline, case, spacing, line height, align, colour, opacity, `fx`, and the optional `fit` / `minSize`) live in `lib/text-style.ts` (`TextStyle`, `sanitizeTextStyle`, `Effects`); `BackEl` extends `TextStyle`, so the overlay can share the model. Sizes are stored as % of the card width and shown in **pt** as if on a 2.5 in card (`pctToPt`, 1 % = 1.8 pt), with a slider and presets (Tiny 6 to Huge 32); arrow keys step 0.5 pt, Shift 5. **Text boxes and paragraph** (`TextBox` in `lib/text-style.ts`, all optional, omitted at their defaults so an old back renders as before, clamped in `sanitizeTextBox` / `sanitizeTextStyle`): `h` (height, % of the card, drag the bottom handle on the canvas), `valign` (top, middle, bottom), `maxLines` (1 to 20, the last line ends with an ellipsis), `wrap: false`, `pad` (% of the card width), `indent` (first line of a paragraph, em), `paraGap` (space between paragraphs, em) and `align: 'justify'`. A text may contain line breaks; a paragraph is the text between them. **Line breaking and auto-fit** are computed once, in `layoutText` (`lib/card-back-layout.ts`), from a measurer (the browser canvas, or a 0.55 em estimate where there is none, such as the overlay's server side). It breaks words greedily into the box width, cuts to `maxLines` and to what fits the box height, and returns explicit lines (`BackNode.lines`) that the DOM back (`FlipCard`), the share canvas (`lib/card-back-canvas.ts`, `ShareImage`) and the overlay all draw as given, so they wrap alike; justified lines are stretched with `text-align-last` in the DOM and by word spacing on the canvas. A free text with none of these fields is left to CSS as before. `fit: 'shrink'` = the largest size up to the set size (down to `minSize`) where the wrapped text fits the width, the height and max lines; `fit: 'width'` = one line as wide as the box. `FlipCard` and the share image lay out again once the fonts have loaded. The overlay lays out on the server with the estimate, so `public/widget.js` (TEXT-FIT markers) holds a port of `layoutText` and runs it again with a canvas measure after the faces load; `scripts/check-card-back.mts` runs both on the same cases.

**Effects and the background plate** (`Effects` in `lib/text-style.ts`, drawn from `effectParts` in `lib/card-back-layout.ts`). Each effect has an on/off switch in the Properties list (off keeps the settings, `on: false`) and several can be on at once: **stroke** (`pos` outside or centre; the DOM uses `-webkit-text-stroke` with `paint-order: stroke fill` for outside, the canvas strokes before the fill for outside and after it for centre, at the same width), **outer glow**, **drop shadows** (up to 2: colour, angle, distance, blur, opacity, stored as x/y offsets), **inner shadow**, **bevel / emboss** (`pressed` or `raised`, depth 0.5 to 3; pressed at depth 1 is the old engrave, byte for byte) and the **background plate** (free text only: colour, padding and corner radius in % of the card width, optional outline; the plate pad is added to the box pad). Old shapes are mapped on read (`outline` to `stroke` outside, `shadow` to `shadows[0]`, `engrave` to a pressed bevel), so an old back lays out identically. The DOM and overlay take everything from `BackNode.css` (the widget copies every css key, no widget change). The inner shadow, and drop shadows on gradient text, go through an SVG data-URI `filter` (`shadeFilter`), with the plain values also on the node (`inner`, `shadow`, `plate`) for the canvas, which draws the inner shadow with an offscreen composite. Gradient text sets `text-shadow: none`, because the stylesheet's engraved highlight was painted over the clipped gradient and washed it out in the DOM. Canvas shadow offsets and blur are multiplied by the context scale. Not supported: a plate on stack elements; qr and logo take only gradient, stroke, glow and shadows (as `drop-shadow` filters).

**Text on a curve** (`arc` in `TextStyle`, -100 to 100, 0 and absent are straight; 100 is a half circle, positive arches up, negative dips). Free text only, and always one line: wrapping, max lines, box height, auto-fit and justify are ignored (the editor disables the fit buttons), newlines become spaces, and the plate, inner shadow and text decoration are dropped. `arcGeometry` in `lib/card-back-layout.ts` computes the path once on the server (radius `w / (2 sin(theta/2))`, `theta = |arc|/100 * PI`, the box height, the text offset by alignment) and ships it as `BackNode.arc`; the DOM (`ArcText` in `FlipCard.tsx`) and the overlay (`backArcSvg` in `public/widget.js`) draw an inline SVG `<text><textPath>` from it, so there are no separate path maths in the widget. Stroke (`paint-order`), the gradient fill (a userSpace `linearGradient`) and shadows (an SVG `drop-shadow` filter, blur halved) are kept. The canvas (`arcText` in `ShareImage.tsx`) places each glyph on the same arc by measured prefix width. Checked in `scripts/check-card-back.mts` (path radius and start for a known width, widget parity).

**Text style presets** (`lib/text-presets.ts` pure: `TextPreset { id, name, style }`, `BUILTIN_TEXT_PRESETS`, `sanitizePresets`, `presetPatch`; `lib/text-presets-store.ts`: KV key `textpresets`, staff-wide; `app/admin/text-preset-actions.ts`: list / save / delete, all `requireAdmin()`). A preset holds only the `TextStyle` fields (font, size, character, colour, effects), never the text, position or box size. The **Style** row at the top of the card back editor's Character section applies a preset to the selection (one undo step; fields the preset leaves unset are cleared), saves the selected text's look under a name ("Save as preset...", 1 to 40 chars, the same name in any case replaces it, at most 50) and deletes a saved one. Styles are run through `sanitizeTextStyle` on save and on read. Four built-ins (Gold title, Fine print, Outlined label, Plate caption) live in code and cannot be deleted. The actions and types do not depend on the card back editor, so the overlay editor can use them later. Checked in `scripts/check-text-presets.mts`.

**Character and colour** (all optional on a free text, omitted at their defaults, clamped in `sanitizeTextStyle`): `strike` and `overline` (with `underline`, one `text-decoration`), `textCase` (`upper`, `lower`, `title`, `smallcaps`; an old `uppercase: true` reads as `upper`, `caseOf`), `tracking` (thousandths of an em, -100 to 800; replaces the old `letterSpacing` tenths when present, `spacingEm`), `wordSpacing` (thousandths of an em), `autoLeading` (line height 1.2 instead of `lineHeight`), `baseline` (% of the size) and `script` (`sup` / `sub`: 0.65 of the size, shifted), `kerning: false`. Baseline and script move the whole box (`top`), so they are for one-line tokens. Lower and title case rewrite the text in `layoutBack`; small caps and upper keep it and draw lowercase letters as capitals at 0.8 of the size (`capRuns`: the DOM wraps those runs in a smaller span, the canvas draws each run at its size, the widget does the same), and small caps always get explicit lines. Word spacing, small caps and kerning change widths, so `layoutText`'s spec carries them and the measurer is told when kerning is off (canvas `fontKerning`). The canvas draws strikethrough and overline as lines, and reads `word-spacing`, `font-kerning` and the decoration from the node's CSS. `fx.gradientText` may carry `stops` (2 to 4 colours with a position in %, sorted; `from` and `to` follow the ends); CSS and the canvas gradient use every stop. The panel keeps these in a collapsible Character section, with a dot beside each changed field that resets it. Italic is labelled "Slanted" for a face with no true italic.

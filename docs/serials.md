# Card serials (unique copies)

> Read this when you touch the copy ledger, serial numbers, unique copy numbers, copy pages, flip cards or share images of copies.

## Card serials (unique copies)
**Wording (ROADMAP 2.6, decided 2026-10-16):** in UI text a copy is a **print**, its game-wide number is the **print number** (`Print #1023381`), `#7` is the **edition** ("Edition #7 of 31") and "serial bonus" is the **edition bonus**. "Copy", "serial" and `uid` below are the code-level names (types, KV keys, URLs such as `/copy/...`, `/c/<uid>` and its alias `/p/<uid>`). `scripts/check-words.mts` bans "unique copy number", "unique number", "copy page" and "ID #" in user-facing source. The Twitch panel still shows the old wording until the next extension version.
Every copy of a card has an identity: `<card>.<variant index>.<n>` (e.g. `7.3.12`), where **n counts up per card AND version** (the first Shiny of a card is Shiny #1, independent of its Regulars). Collections are unchanged (`number[]` per viewer, source of truth for scores etc.); a **ledger** sits beside them.
- **Pure** `lib/serials-core.ts` (ids, `diffCounts`, `newestFirst`, history events, labels) and **server** `lib/serials.ts` (KV per collection name: `copies:<c>:o:<owner>` hash of Copy records, `copies:<c>:idx` id → owner, `copies:<c>:n` entry → ever made = next serial, `copies:<c>:ret` retired copies with history, `copies:<c>:init` flag). An owner is a viewer or `escrow:<tradeId>`.
- **One choke point**: `saveCollection(name, data, ctx?)` in `lib/kv.ts` reads the previous blob (only when tracking) and calls `syncAfterSave`, which mints what was added, retires what was removed (newest serial first, history kept) and never throws. `ctx.origin` says where new copies came from (`addCollectionEntry(..., origin)`: `'pull'` for the overlay, `'grant'` for admin / webhook grants); `ctx.moves` makes trades keep identity (offer → escrow, escrow → taker, taker → offerer, refund → offerer; the **newest** copies move first); `ctx.upgrade` retires the spent copies (`upgraded`) and mints the new one with `madeFrom` pointing back. New code that changes collections just calls `saveCollection`; pass `moves` only if copies must keep their identity.
- **Tracking is off per collection until `backfillSerials`** (Admin → Settings → Twitch & viewers → Card prints → "Number existing prints", under the collection lock, safe to repeat). Existing copies get estimated numbers (`est`, flagged "~" / "estimated number"); everything after is exact. Sandboxes don't copy the ledger, so a sandbox needs its own backfill.
- **Surfaces**: serial chips in the profile card detail (`GridCard.copies`), the public copy page `/copy/<seasonId>/<copyId>` (card, "#n of N made", holder, origin, full history; viewers an admin hid or who made their profile private are shown as "a private collector"; noindex), and **share images** (`lib/share-image.ts` layout, `app/components/ShareImage.tsx` draws a 1080×1350 PNG on a canvas in the browser and shares or downloads it): "Share my cards" on a profile (best 6 cards with version and lowest serial) and "Share this card" on a copy page. History keeps the first event plus the latest 40. **The copy page's share image** has an engraved steel **plate** under the card (`ShareSpec.plate`, `SharePlate` in `lib/share-image.ts`; drawn by `drawPlate` in `ShareImage.tsx`): the unique copy number (gold when it earns a bonus), the edition line, the pulled / numbered date, who holds it (masked for private collectors) and a QR code to the copy (`lib/qr.ts`, shared with the flip card); the footer is the short link `/c/<number>`. The card shrinks to leave room (`cardAreaWithPlate` / `plateRect`; layout checked in `check-serials.mts`). Other share images (profile, stats, Card Dex) have no plate.
- **Browsing serials**: copies of a card + version are numbered 1..made with none skipped, so a page of them is just a range of ids (`pageCopies(c, card, variant, page, size)` in `lib/serials.ts`, live and retired together; `liveCounts` tallies live copies per entry). **Admin → Serials** (`/admin/serials`, nav key `n`, a GET-filtered server page: season, card, version, holder via `UserInput`; the overview grid shows made (held) per card and version, clicking a cell lists its copies newest first, a holder lists everything they hold) and **`/admin/serials/[seasonId]/[copyId]`** (unmasked details, `madeFrom` links, full history, and two actions in `app/admin/serial-actions.ts`: **Give to another viewer** and **Remove from the holder's collection**, both under the collection lock, both refused while the copy is in a trade offer, both leaving the collection array and the ledger in step). To act on one exact copy, `saveCollection`'s `SyncContext` takes `moves[].ids` / `note` (move those copies instead of the newest `count`) and `retire: { owner, ids, who, note }` (retire exactly those). Public: the Card Dex dialog's **Browse every copy** link opens the page `/cards/[card]/copies?season=&variant=&page=` (server-rendered from `pageCopies`: a tab per version, 25 per page newest first; holders an admin hid or who went private show as a private collector, a copy in an offer as "in a trade offer", a retired one with how it ended), and each row links to the public copy page. The dialog's **Share this card** button sits under the art, below "Showing: <version>".
- **Unique copy numbers (`Copy.uid`)**: on top of the edition number `n` (per card + version, drives the points bonus) every copy has a number that is unique across the whole game, e.g. `#1023381`. Handed out by a global counter (KV hash `copies:uid:seq`, `UID_BASE` = 1,000,000 so they are all 7 digits) inside `mint()`, kept by the copy through trades (it is the same record), new for an upgrade's result, retained after retirement. `copies:uid:idx` maps uid -> `<collection>|<copy id>` (`findByUid()`), so `/c/<uid>` redirects to `/copy/<season>/<copyId>` for any season, and the site search recognises a typed `#1023381` (`parseUid()`, 7-9 digits only, a `copy` result kind). **Existing copies**: `backfillSerials` numbers new ledger entries without a uid and then `assignMissingUids(c)` gives every copy that has none (live and retired) a number, oldest first (`at`, then card, version, edition); the Settings -> Twitch & viewers -> Card prints panel has **Give print numbers** for collections that are already tracked (safe to repeat). Counters are per sandbox like all keys. Shown: copy page (big number + "Edition #n of N"), the engraved flip-card back (the uid is the engraved number, the edition under it), the card dialog's serial list ("ID #..."), admin copy page. The overlay pill and activity feed still show the edition (`#7`).
- **Showcase copy (profile widget `pinned`)**: the owner pins one copy of theirs (`UserSettings.pinned` = its unique number; sanitised to a plausible uid, **checked against the live owner every time it is shown**, so a copy traded away simply stops being shown). Pinned from the copy page's **Pin to my profile** button (`PinCopyButton`, only for the holder; `pinCopyAction` in `app/(entity)/copy/actions.ts`: viewer from the session, the copy must be theirs, and the widget is added to their layout via `withPinnedWidget`) and unpinned from the same button or the widget. The widget shows the copy as a **FlipCard** (engraved unique number on the back, QR, journey link), its number and edition, and a **Share this copy** button. `loadCopyByUid` (`lib/copy-view.ts`) finds the copy + season + card from a unique number (any season); the copy's share image spec is built in one place, `copyShareSpec` (`lib/copy-share.ts`), used by the copy page and the widget. Checks in `scripts/check-user-settings.mts`.
- **Journey (copy page)**: the plain history list is now a provenance timeline. Pure `lib/provenance.ts` (`buildProvenance(copy, now)` -> steps made / offered / passed / returned / forged / removed with who held it after each step and for how long (time in a trade offer is nobody's), the holder list, completed trades, the longest hold, age (stops when the copy is spent) and state; `spanText`; `scripts/check-provenance.mts`) and the server component `app/components/ProvenanceTimeline.tsx` (summary tiles, a stacked "who held it" bar, the forged-from / forged-into links by unique number, the step list). Names are masked by the page (`who` / `plain`), times shown in UTC, and the copy keeps only its first event plus the latest 40, so the middle of a very busy copy is missing (the page says so).
- **Flip card** (`app/components/FlipCard.tsx`, client; pure back data in `lib/flip-back.ts`: `FlipBack`, `engravedDate` (UTC, so server and browser print the same), `madeLine`; CSS `.flip-*` in `globals.css`): the front is whatever is passed as children (the normal art tile with its holo), the back is an engraved light-steel plate with the serial (gold when it earns a bonus), "of N made", version, the date, origin and holder, and (copy page only) the last 3 history lines plus a QR code (`qrcode-generator`, drawn as one SVG path) and (card dialog) a "Full history" link. Pointer tilt (±12°, glare follows it; `touch-action: pan-y` so scrolling still works), tap / the real **Flip** button turns it over (the button is the keyboard route; the card itself is not a button because the back holds a link). Reduced motion (device setting or `data-motion="reduce"`) swaps the turn and tilt for a fade. Used on `/copy/<season>/<copy>` and in the profile card dialog (back = the lowest-numbered copy of the version shown; `GridCard.copies` carries `at`, `origin`, `made` for it). Holders are masked the same way as on the copy page. `scripts/check-flip-back.mts` covers the date and wording.
- `scripts/check-serials.mts` (mock KV) covers ids, backfill, per-version numbering, removals, trade identity, refunds, upgrades, layout and production isolation.
- **Serials are shown prominently everywhere** (`SerialChip`, `app/components/SerialChip.tsx`: `#7`, gold with the multiplier when it earns a bonus, a gradient "first ever" for #1): on the **overlay reveal** (a pill top-left of the card opposite the label, `showSerial()` in `widget.js`, property `reveal.showSerial`; the number comes from `POST /api/collection` which now returns `{ serial, serialMult }` via `addCollectionEntryWithSerial` / `lastSerial`, read under the collection lock so it is the copy just made; dry runs and the editor still show a sample), in the **pull activity events** (`serial` + `bonus` on the `pull` event: the home Recent pulls, the admin feed and event detail, the plain-text line, the Discord embed's Serial field), on the **collection tiles** (lowest serial of the version shown) and in the card dialog's **prints tiles** (one tile per print with its edition chip and `Print #uid`; see "Prints-first card dialog" in docs/public-site.md; `GridCard.copies[].m` / `.p` / `.j` come from `buildGridCards`), on the **copy page** (big number, "Worth N pts instead of M" and the bonus), in the **upgrade ceremony** (`upgradeCard` returns the new copy's `serial` / `serialMult`; web and panel), on the **trade stacks** (`TradeStacks` `pool`: the serials that would move, newest first) and as a mark on the **Twitch panel tiles**.
- **Profile Recent pulls (ROADMAP 2.3, widget id `recent`)**: the owner's latest 8 prints whose origin is `pull` or `grant` (webhook rewards and admin gifts, labelled "Granted" while still held) and whose first event names them (`pickRecentPulls` in `lib/recent-pulls-core.ts`), whether still held, in an offer, traded away, upgraded or removed (`pullFate`). Candidates are the prints they hold plus the ones their `pull` and `grant` activity events name (`copyId`, or derived from the edition), read with `getCopy` (live or retired). Each row: card + version, `Print #n` + what became of it, edition chip, age, opening the print. Untracked collection: the owner sees their pull events (no numbers), visitors see nothing. A hidden profile only shows prints it still holds (its events are not read).

- **Events point at the print (ROADMAP 2.1, 2.2)**: `pull`, `grant` and `upgrade` activity events carry `copyId` / `uid` (older pulls only have `serial`, so the id is derived). `copyHrefFor` / `adminCopyHrefFor` in `lib/copy-link.ts` give the link: the home Recent pulls (`PullItem.href` / `.uid`, "Print #..." under the edition chip) and the admin and mod activity feeds (card name, plus an "Open this print" button in the event detail; admin goes to `/admin/serials/...`, mods to the public `/c/<uid>`) use it. The feed gets a collection -> season id map (`PrintSeasons`) because events only know their collection.

- **Print of the week (ROADMAP 6.2)**: a print with an interesting journey, featured on the home page. Pure `lib/print-of-week.ts`: `scorePrint(copy, now)` (distinct holders x3, completed trades x2 from `buildProvenance`, edition #1 +5, Shiny / Full Art +2, age in weeks capped at 8, forged +2; null for a spent print, one in a trade offer or one without a print number), `pickPrintOfWeek(candidates, recentUids)` (highest score, the older print on a tie, nothing picked in the last 8 weeks), `isoWeekKey`. Server `lib/print-of-week-store.ts`: KV `potw:current` (`PotwPick`: uid, seasonId, copyId, score, reason, chosenAt, `by` = 'auto' or the staff login), `potw:history` (newest 20 `{uid, at}`), `potw:week:<yyyy-Www>` (NX lock, 21 days). `scanSeason` reads every viewer in the active season's collection blob and their ledger hash (about 0.5 s for 15,000 prints / 313 viewers on the mock KV; real KV adds a round trip per batch of 10 viewers). `pickOncePerWeek` is the weekly cron's step (docs/hosting.md); `pickNow` is the admin **Pick again now** (Admin -> Prints, with **Clear**); **Feature as print of the week** sits on `/admin/serials/<season>/<copy>`. All admin actions are in `app/admin/serial-actions.ts` and write an audit entry for the holder. Home: `PrintOfWeek` shows the flip card, `Print #...`, the edition line, the reason, the holder (masked like the print page) and a link to its journey; `loadFeatured` returns null when there is no pick or the print was spent (owner gone) or renumbered, so the section just disappears. `scripts/check-print-of-week.mts` covers scoring, the skip rules, the cron (secret, production only, sandbox, once a week) and the store.
- **Profile Rarest prints and Showcase (ROADMAP 8.1, 8.2)**: the `rarest` widget lists the holder's 5 rarest prints (`rarestPrints` in `lib/collection-view.ts`: one per card + version, their lowest edition of it, ordered by the chance of that card in that version = `pullRate` x the version's share of `maths.weights`, ties to the lower edition), each opening `/c/<uid>`; an untracked season keeps the card-level list. The `showcase` tiles open the holder's best print (`bestPrintOf`) of the card + version that earned the place.

- **Admin Prints screen (ROADMAP 10.5)**: `/admin/serials` is one list driven by the URL (`?season=&card=&v=&holder=&origin=&q=&page=&print=`, so a view can be shared between staff). Top: tiles (made, held, in offers via `inOfferCount`, retired, last numbered via `lastUid`, numbering status) and one search box (`parsePrintSearch` in `lib/serials-view.ts`: `#1023381` / `1023381` jumps to that print through `findByUid`, an exact viewer login lists their prints, otherwise a card name or number narrows the list). Main: a card list (art, rarity, made (held) per version); opening a card shows its editions as chips (edition, print number, holder, a star when the edition earns a bonus), oldest first, with version tabs, a holder filter (a login, `offer` or `retired`) and an origin filter (`filterEditions`; with a filter the first 600 editions are read, otherwise 120 per page). A chip opens `?print=<copyId>`: a side panel on desktop, a full-screen sheet on phones, closed with a plain link so the view keeps its filters. The panel is `PrintDetail` (`app/admin/serials/PrintDetail.tsx`: details, Give, Feature as print of the week, Remove, history), the same component the deep-link page `/admin/serials/<season>/<copy>` uses. The panel's forms carry a hidden `ret` (checked by `safeSerialsReturn`: only `/admin/serials?...`), so the actions in `serial-actions.ts` come back to the list with the panel open; without it they come back to the print's page. Check: `scripts/check-serials-view.mts`.

# Trading

> Read this when you touch trades, escrow, the trade pages, notifications, listing alerts, the trade ledger audit or the Twitch panel Trades tab.

- **daisyUI on the public trade pages:** `TradeCard` and each side of the composer are daisyUI `card` (the navy surface is part of `card`). The board search and sort, the "offer to" name, the note and the card search use `input`, `select` and `fieldset`. Cheese `.rind` sections, the board filter chips, the lopsided-offer tick, print chips and the second-tap actions stay as they are. This is styling only: escrow, locks and the engine are unchanged. The Twitch panel Trades tab is frozen and was not restyled.

## Trading
- **Trading (website + the Twitch panel's Trades tab)**: direct offers to one viewer and an open board of listings, any copy in any variant. **Escrow**: offering removes the copies from the offerer's collection into the trade (`Trade.escrowed`), so collections + escrow is conserved; accept swaps (`settleTrade`), decline / cancel / expiry / counter refund. KV has no multi-key transaction, so an offer is saved as closed (still `escrowed: true`) **before** its copies move, and the flag is cleared after (`closeTrade`, `acceptTrade`): a failure in between leaves a closed offer stuck in escrow (Data health lists it and **Return prints** / `releaseStuckEscrow` puts back only what that offer's escrow still holds, then clears the flag; a second run does not add prints again; a deleted season refuses and leaves the flag set), never one refunded twice. A sweep inside create or accept saves that cleared flag before the call returns, including when the offer itself is then refused. If the season record is already gone, the refund does not run and `escrowed` stays true. `deleteSeason` is refused while `trades:open` still has an offer for that season. Escrowed copies don't count towards score while open. Storage in KV: `trades:open`, `trades:history` (capped 400), `trade:settings`, `trade:cd:<user>`. Pure logic in `lib/trade-core.ts` (client-safe), engine in `lib/trades.ts`, every mutation under `withLock('trades')` then a collection lock (always that order). **No background job**: expired offers are refunded lazily by `sweepTrades()`, called from `/trade`, `/trade/new`, `/account`, `/collection/[username]` and `/admin/trades`; call it from any new page that shows collections. The viewer always comes from the session (`requireViewer` in `lib/trade-api.ts`), never the body. API: `POST /api/trades`, `POST /api/trades/[id]` (`accept|decline|cancel|counter`), `GET /api/trades/holdings` (a signed-in viewer or staff; a private profile only for that viewer and staff). Pages: `/trade`, `/trade/new[?to=|?counter=]`, `/admin/trades` (settings, pause switch, cancelling still works while paused, , open offers with cancel & refund, log). Settings: `TradeSettings` (expiry 48h, 5 open offers, 20s cooldown, 8 stacks per side). **Notifications**: every outcome notifies whoever needs to know (`lib/trade-notify.ts`: new offer / counter → recipient; accepted / declined / admin cancel / expired → offerer; withdrawn / expired → recipient) into the KV list `notif:<user>` (30 newest) with `notif:<user>:seen` marking what is read; `GET/POST /api/notifications` (session only) feed the **bell** in `SiteNav` (`NotificationBell`; every `notify()` and mark-as-read bumps `notif:<user>:v`, the bell polls `GET /api/notifications?since=<v>` which answers 204 when nothing changed: every 10s while the tab is visible, 60s after 5 minutes without input, not while hidden, at once on focus or after a trade action (`tcg:notices-check` event from `postJson`). A new notice toasts (portal on `<body>`), shakes the bell, prefixes the tab title with `(N)` and sets the app badge; `UserSettings.noticeSound` adds a chime. **Web Push** (ROADMAP 4.3): `notify()` also calls `pushNotice()` (`lib/push.ts`, the only importer of `web-push`; pure shapes and validation in `lib/push-core.ts`, browser half in `lib/push-client.ts`), which sends through `after()` when VAPID keys are set, not in a sandbox, the bell switch (`wantsNotice`) allows the notice and `UserSettings.pushKinds` (offers / results / watch / admin, all on by default) does too. Devices are the KV hash `push:<user>` (max 5, oldest dropped; a 404 / 410 from the push service removes one); `GET/POST/DELETE /api/account/push` (session only, `isSameOrigin`). Requests are signed with `generateRequestDetails` and sent with `fetch`, so a plain-http localhost endpoint works outside production for `scripts/mock-push.cjs` / `scripts/check-push.mts`. `public/sw.js` shows the alert and opens `data.url` on click; Settings -> Notifications (`PushAlerts`) and a once-only toast offer in `NotificationBell` (`localStorage` `tcg_push_offered`, only after an offer or counter arrives) turn it on. Without `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` / `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` the whole option is hidden. **Email** (ROADMAP 22.1): `notify()` then calls `emailNotice()` (`lib/email.ts`, the only file that talks to Resend, with `fetch`; pure shapes, address rules and the email text in `lib/email-core.ts`). The record is `user:<u>:email` `{ address, addedAt, verifiedAt?, kinds, off? }` (kinds = the push groups, default offers and results). Double opt-in: `POST /api/account/email { address }` stores it unconfirmed and mails a signed 24 h link (`/api/email/confirm?t=`, HMAC with `SESSION_SECRET`, tied to a hash of the address so a changed address kills old links); nothing else is sent before that. A notice for a kind the record wants (and the bell switch allows) joins the queue `email:q:<u>`; one email per viewer per 15 minutes (the window is the key `email:last:<u>`, SET NX EX), which takes the whole queue (a burst is one email, subject like "3 new trade offers"). The first notice waits 20 s in `after()` to catch a burst; notices inside the window wait for the next notice or for `flushDueEmails()` in the daily backup cron (`email:pending` set). Every notification email has `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and a link to `/api/email/unsubscribe?t=` (GET or one-click POST, signed, no expiry, only switches off). `POST /api/webhooks/email` takes Resend's bounce / complaint events (Svix signature with `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; the `viewer` tag on each send says whose it was) and sets `off`. Turning it on again needs a new confirmation. Silent in sandboxes and previews; included in the data export and removed with the account. Without `RESEND_API_KEY` (and `SESSION_SECRET`) the Email section (`EmailAlerts`) is hidden. `RESEND_API_URL` points the sender at `scripts/mock-resend.cjs` outside production; `scripts/check-email.mts`. **Discord DMs** (ROADMAP 4.4): `notify()` also calls `discordNotice()` (`lib/discord-user.ts`, pure shapes and payload formatting in `lib/discord-dm-core.ts`), sending through `after()` when Discord bot credentials (`DISCORD_CLIENT_ID`, `DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET`, `DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN`) are configured and not in a sandbox. Account linking runs Discord OAuth with `identify` scope at `/auth/discord` -> `/auth/discord/callback` (the access token is revoked immediately and never stored; the user ID is kept in `user:<u>:discord` and `discord:user:<discordId>`). In Settings -> Notifications (`DiscordAlerts`), viewers choose which kinds of notice send DMs (offers, results, watch, admin). When Discord direct messages are blocked (error 50007), `dmStatus` is marked `closed` and Settings advises allowing server member DMs. Rate limits (429) retry once using `retry_after`. Included in data export and cleaned up on account deletion; tested in `scripts/check-discord.mts`. Pure rules in `lib/notice-poll-core.ts`, `scripts/check-notice-poll.mts`). The engine queues events while it holds the trade lock and sends them after it is released (`queueEvent` / `flushEvents` in `lib/trades.ts`; building them reads the season, which must not hold the lock). Add new outcomes through `closeTrade` / `createInLock` so they are announced. **Trade animation** (`TradeCeremony.tsx`, `.tr-*` CSS): plays for the accepter when `POST /api/trades/[id]` returns `swap`, and for the offerer when the bell finds a fresh `accepted` notice (once, remembered in `localStorage` `tcg_trade_played`; "Replay" in the bell). Cards keep their holo (`SwapCard.holo`, via `buildSwap`). **Activity / Discord**: every action (offered, countered, accepted, declined, cancelled, expired) is a `trade` activity event carrying both sides' cards as readable lines plus points, the note and kind; Discord posts offers, counters, completed trades and admin cancels (not declines / withdrawals / expiries) as an embed with a field per side. The card popup on someone else's collection has an **Offer a trade** button (`/trade/new?to=<user>&want=<card>.<variant>` pre-selects the card you ask for). Sandbox-testable like everything on `kv`. `scripts/check-trades.mts` (needs the mock KV) checks conservation, races, expiry, caps, bulk cancel / extend, the minimum account age, a sweep that is then refused, a refund after the season record is gone, and returning a stuck offer once. **Admin Trades page**: `lib/trade-insights.ts` (client-safe, pure) computes the stats (`computeTradeStats`, from open + the kept 400-trade history, so it describes recent activity) and the flags (`flagTrades`: lopsided = one side ≥ 2× the other and ≥ 50 points apart at today's values, repeat pair = 3+ completed trades between two viewers in 7 days, rapid offers = 5+ in 10 minutes, new account = profile under 24h old when offered; thresholds in `FLAG_RULES`). Flags are advisory; "Block from trading" uses the Users moderation flag (`saveFlags`) and cancels that viewer's open offers. `TradeSettings.minAccountHours` (0 = off) stops a profile younger than that offering or accepting. Bulk engine functions `cancelOpenTrades` / `extendOpenTrades` run in one locked pass and announce through `closeTrade`.
- **Already-affected offers (read-only audit)**: `scripts/audit-trade-ledger.mts` only reads (`get` / `hgetall`). It does not sweep, lock, or write. Prefer the read-only token. There is no default URL, so a missing env does not hit localhost. Exit 0 after a successful read, including when rows match. Exit 1 when the URL or token is missing. `--json` prints the report object. The host is printed, never the token. Finished offers outside the kept 400 are invisible.
  ```
  KV_REST_API_URL=... KV_REST_API_READ_ONLY_TOKEN=... KV_URL=redis://unused npx tsx scripts/audit-trade-ledger.mts
  ```
  `KV_REST_API_TOKEN` is used only when `KV_REST_API_READ_ONLY_TOKEN` is unset. The first three sections are the checks that mean a record was already affected:
  - `already refunded, still marked escrowed`: closed, `escrowed` still true, the escrow hash holds none of the give side, and some print of that give side has a `returned` event for the offerer from 5 seconds before `resolvedAt` to 2 minutes after. An empty escrow hash on its own is not this row.
  - `open offers for a deleted season`: `trades:open` whose `seasonId` is not in `seasons:list`. Not cancelled by this script.
  - `possible restore duplicates`: a second set may have been minted. Three patterns, and a normal pull (`origin: pull`) is not one of them: the offerer holds an `other` print minted at or after an open offer while that offer's escrow still holds the card; the viewer holds `other` prints of a card and at least as many retired prints of it were `removed` from them first; the viewer holds prints that `returned` to them and a later `other` print of the same card. Confirm before removing anything. The repair never deletes prints.
  `None of the three checks matched.` means those three sections are empty. `N rows matched the three checks.` means look at them. Sections after `The rows below are not counted as already-affected` (still in escrow, partial, escrow empty but the refund is not confirmed, unnumbered, closed offer whose season is missing) need a person and are not counted.
  `scripts/repair-trade-ledger.mts` plans the same report and writes nothing unless both switches are set. It needs the read-write `KV_REST_API_TOKEN`, not the read-only one (`--apply` refuses when the two tokens are the same value). Dry-run:
  ```
  KV_REST_API_URL=... KV_REST_API_TOKEN=... KV_URL=redis://unused npx tsx scripts/repair-trade-ledger.mts
  ```
  Apply, after reading the dry-run:
  ```
  TCG_REPAIR_CONFIRM=yes npx tsx scripts/repair-trade-ledger.mts --apply
  ```
  Safe writes only: `clear-flag` (`clearEscrowFlagIfEmpty`, no cards appended; the collection scan is before the trades lock, and the lock checks the escrow hash is still empty) and `return-prints` (`releaseStuckEscrow`, numbered seasons, prints still in escrow). `left for a person` is not applied. `Dry run. No keys were written.` and `Refusing to write` mean nothing changed. `scripts/check-trade-ledger-audit.mts` covers the classifier and a local mock run, and refuses any KV that is not localhost.
- **Listing alerts (watches)**: `UserSettings.watches` (up to `WATCH_MAX` = 10 `{ card, variant | null }`, "tell me when someone lists a Shiny Gouda"; edited in Settings -> Saved alerts, anchor `#watches`) plus the existing Looking for list (any version) are the viewer's alerts; `notifyWatch` (default on) switches the notices off (`'watch'` notice group). Pure `lib/watch-core.ts` (`matchingAlerts`, `alertCards`, `watchLabel`); server `lib/watch-index.ts`: a KV set per card number `watch:idx:<card>` of the viewers who want it, kept up to date by `saveUserSettings` (`syncWatchIndex`) and built on first use from the viewers who have played (`watch:idx:ready`; no key scan, sandboxes cannot scan). A new board **listing** (not a direct offer or counter) goes through `announceTrade` -> `notifyWatchers` (lib/trade-notify.ts): every matching viewer except the lister gets one `watch` bell notice (cap 200 per listing; bell link "See the listing" -> `/trade?tab=board`). The website's Matches panel treats a watched card as wanted (card-level; a variant-specific watch also matches other versions there). **Sanitiser gotcha**: `sanitizeUserSettings` must give every array a fresh copy (`experiments`, `watches`), never push into the shared `DEFAULT_SETTINGS` ones. **Wanted counts** (ROADMAP 6.1): for each card an open board listing gives, `wantedCounts` (pure, `lib/watch-core.ts`) counts the people on that card's watch list minus the lister and any recipient; `wantedForTrades` (`lib/watch-index.ts`) reads them in one batch for the visible listings. Shown as a purple "N want this" chip in `TradeStacks` (`wanted` prop through `TradeCard`) on `/trade` and `/trade/<id>`, and as a "Wanted" line in the Trade listings Discord embed. Counts only, never names. `scripts/check-watch.mts` (mock KV).
- **Trade page upgrades**: `/trade` has `?tab=` sections (**Your trades** with offers for you, your open offers and **Matches for you**; **The board**; **History**; the default is Your trades when you have open offers, else the board). **Matches** come from the pure `lib/trade-match.ts` (`matchTrade`, `bestMatches`): board listings you can take that give a card on your Looking for list or one you do not own at all, or ask only for your spares (a spare = you would still keep a copy) at no loss, ranked with plain-language reasons. The board has `BoardToolbar` (search a card or collector; I can take / Has a card I want / Has a card I lack / Asks for my spares; sort by newest, ending soon or best for me): listings opt in with `data-board` + `data-*` attributes and the toolbar toggles `hidden` and CSS `order`, no re-render. The composer (`TradeComposer`) adds **quick picks** (cards the other person is looking for that you own, and cards you are looking for that they have; their list comes back from `GET /api/trades/holdings` as `wanted`), a **Spares only** filter on your side and a **fairness label** from `fairness()`; a lopsided offer (you give at least twice as many points and 50+ more) needs a tick before it can be sent. `scripts/check-trade-match.mts` covers the matching and fairness rules. The engine (escrow, locks, serials, notices) is unchanged.
- **Composer states (`/trade/new`)**: a message page (no season, frozen season, trading paused, **trading switched off for this viewer**, a counter of an offer that is gone or **not addressed to the viewer**) keeps the heading (`say()` in the page). A link that names the other person (`?to=`, `?counter=`, `?want=`) loads their cards at once (`TradeComposer` runs `lookUp()` on mount when `initial.to` is set).
- **Second tap on every action (`TradeActions`)**: Accept / Take this trade / Decline / Cancel offer each need a second tap, like the Twitch panel: the first tap turns the button into "Sure?" with a short "Tap again to ..." line and it relaxes after 4 seconds; each offer card keeps its own armed state. Used on `/trade` and on the profile Trades tab.
- **Trade page (`/trade/[id]`, in the `(entity)` group: real 404, noindex)**: one offer on its own address, the link Discord listing posts use. It runs `sweepTrades()`, reads the open trade or its history entry, and shows `TradeCard` (stacks with serials, lister, expiry) plus, for the signed-in viewer, a fairness line from their side and the same `TradeActions` second-tap flow (lister: Cancel offer; recipient of a direct offer: Accept / Counter / Decline; anyone else on a listing: Take this trade). Signed out: "Log in with Twitch" with `returnTo` back here. A **direct offer is private**: `tradeVisibleTo(trade, viewer)` (lib/trade-core.ts) lets only its two people see it, everyone else gets "This is a private offer". A closed trade shows its outcome and no actions. Board and Matches cards link to it ("Open").
- **Listing posts in Discord**: a new board listing (not a direct offer or counter) also calls `notifyListing()` (lib/discord.ts) from `announceTrade`, for channels with **Trade listings** on: "<lister> listed a trade", Gives (with the edition numbers of the copies that move) / Wants, points, an end time (`<t:...:R>`), the first card's art, and the title linking to `/trade/<id>`. Mentions stay empty and the post is never edited when the listing is taken or ends (the trade page shows the outcome). Silent in a sandbox.
- **Choosing which print to trade (ROADMAP 6.4)**: `TradeItem.ids?` (copy ids, `ids.length === count`, all distinct and of that card + version; `cleanIds` / `cleanItems` in `lib/trade-core.ts`) names exact prints; absent = any print, the newest first, exactly as before (old trades, the Twitch panel and unpicked asks all keep working). **Give side**: `createInLock` checks inside the collection lock that every id is held by the offerer right now (`firstNotHeld`, `lib/serials.ts`: not sold, forged, or already in another offer's escrow) and escrow / settle / refund pass `moveOf(item)` (`moves[].ids`) so the same prints go out and come back. **Get side**: a direct offer may pin the recipient's prints (checked held by them at offer time, and for a counter also in the escrow of the offer it answers; a **listing refuses ids on `get`**, a stranger picks at accept). `acceptTrade(id, user, getIds?)` takes `getIds` (`POST /api/trades/[id]` accept body, `{ "<collection entry>": ["<print id>", ...] }`, the entry being `encodeEntry(card, variant)`); `withChosenPrints` fills the taker's picks into the stacks the offerer did not pin; every picked print is re-checked held by the taker inside the lock, and if one has moved the accept fails ("That offer asks for Print #1023381, which you no longer hold.") and the offer stays open. The accepted trade's `get` is rewritten with the prints that really moved. A season whose prints are not numbered (`isTracking` false) refuses ids. `stackPoints` (and so fairness, matches, Discord and feed points) prices a picked stack from its own ids, otherwise from the newest of the pool. UI: `GET /api/trades/holdings` also returns `prints` (a `PrintPool`: id, edition `n`, `uid` per entry, `getPrintPool` in `lib/trade-view.ts`); `PrintPicker` (chips, "Use the newest", an Edition #1 / edition-bonus warning when giving) is used by the composer ("Choose prints" on a stack with spare prints, default newest) and by `TradeActions` (`pick` prop from `pickPropsFor`; on the first Accept / Take tap it loads the viewer's prints and, when they have spares of an asked-for card, shows the picker before the second-tap "Sure?"). `TradeStacks` shows picked prints with "Print #uid" (`TradePools.uids`) and "any print" on an open trade's asking side. Twitch panel: unchanged (frozen); its serial preview can differ from what moves for a picked trade until the next extension version.
- **Favourite prints (ROADMAP 9.1)**: `UserSettings.favourites` (up to `FAVOURITES_MAX` = 50 print numbers, `Copy.uid`) are prints that can't be traded or spent in an upgrade. Marked with `FavouriteCopyButton` (print page, the print view of the collection card dialog) through `favouriteCopyAction` (`app/(entity)/copy/actions.ts`: session viewer only, the print must be held, not in escrow). The rule lives in `lib/favourites.ts` and is checked against the live owner each time (a favourite that has left them stops counting): `protectFavourites` runs inside the collection lock in `createInLock` (give side) and `acceptTrade` (taker side). A picked favourite is refused; unpicked stacks are pinned to the newest non-favourite prints (only when the owner has favourites of that card, otherwise the ledger's newest-first is unchanged); "only favourites left" is refused with the matching words. A refused accept leaves the offer open. `upgradeCard` does the same through `spendablePrints` and `SyncContext.upgrade.ids`. Panel trades follow the rule with the existing `{ ok: false, error }` shape (panel frozen). Not yet done: hearts on the composer's print picker and profile tiles. Checks in `scripts/check-trades.mts` (favourites block).
- **Staff reverse a completed trade (ROADMAP 9.2)**: `reverseTrade(id, staff, reason)` in `lib/trades.ts` (admins and mods; a reason is required). Settlement now stores `Trade.moved` (`give` / `get`: the exact print ids, read by `movedPrints` just before they move; absent when the season's prints are not numbered). A trade without it is rebuilt from the ledger (`rebuildMoved`: live copies of the traded cards with a `traded` event between the two people within a few seconds of `resolvedAt`) and only if every stack matches its count exactly; otherwise "This trade is too old to reverse automatically". All or nothing under the trade lock and the collection lock: each print must still be with the person who received it and not in an open offer, else the first offender is named and nothing moves. Prints go back through `saveCollection` moves carrying `reversal`, which writes a `reversed` copy event (who = staff, note = reason; shown in the print journey). The trade keeps `status: 'accepted'` and gains `reversedAt` / `reversedBy` / `reverseReason`; both people get an `admin` bell notice, the activity feed gets a `reversed` trade event (staff named, goes to the Discord admin category), and `recordAudit` logs it per person. UI: `ReverseTrade` (reason field + `ArmedButton`) in the admin / mod trade log detail panel and on `/trade/<id>` for staff (`reverseTradeAction` in `app/admin/trade-actions.ts`); the trade page and the History tab say "Reversed by staff". The Twitch panel is frozen and shows it as completed. Completed-trade counts on profiles still include reversed trades. Checks: `scripts/check-trades.mts` (reversal block).
- **Turn-on-alerts banner (ROADMAP 8.11)**: `PushPrompt` (`app/components/site/PushPrompt.tsx`) sits at the top of `/account`, `/trade` and `/notifications`. For a signed-in viewer on a phone or the installed app, when the server has push keys, push is supported, permission is still `default` and this device has no subscription, it offers "Get trade offers on your phone" with **Turn on alerts** (the same `enablePush` flow as Settings) and **Not now**. "Not now" hides it for 14 days (`localStorage` `tcg_push_prompt`, in try/catch) and after 3 it stops. An iPhone in Safari (`pushSupport()` = `install-first`) is shown "Install the app to get alerts" linking to `/install`; a blocked permission shows nothing. The decision is the pure `promptMode` in `lib/push-prompt-core.ts` (`scripts/check-push-prompt.mts`). The one-off toast after a first offer (`tcg_push_offered`) is unchanged.

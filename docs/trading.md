# Trading

> Read this when you touch trades, escrow, the trade pages, notifications, listing alerts or the Twitch panel Trades tab.

## Trading
- **Trading (website + the Twitch panel's Trades tab)**: direct offers to one viewer and an open board of listings, any copy in any variant. **Escrow**: offering removes the copies from the offerer's collection into the trade (`Trade.escrowed`), so collections + escrow is conserved; accept swaps (`settleTrade`), decline / cancel / expiry / counter refund. Escrowed copies don't count towards score while open. Storage in KV: `trades:open`, `trades:history` (capped 400), `trade:settings`, `trade:cd:<user>`. Pure logic in `lib/trade-core.ts` (client-safe), engine in `lib/trades.ts`, every mutation under `withLock('trades')` then a collection lock (always that order). **No background job**: expired offers are refunded lazily by `sweepTrades()`, called from `/trade`, `/trade/new`, `/account`, `/collection/[username]` and `/admin/trades`; call it from any new page that shows collections. The viewer always comes from the session (`requireViewer` in `lib/trade-api.ts`), never the body. API: `POST /api/trades`, `POST /api/trades/[id]` (`accept|decline|cancel|counter`), `GET /api/trades/holdings`. Pages: `/trade`, `/trade/new[?to=|?counter=]`, `/admin/trades` (settings, pause switch, cancelling still works while paused, , open offers with cancel & refund, log). Settings: `TradeSettings` (expiry 48h, 5 open offers, 20s cooldown, 8 stacks per side). **Notifications**: every outcome notifies whoever needs to know (`lib/trade-notify.ts`: new offer / counter → recipient; accepted / declined / admin cancel / expired → offerer; withdrawn / expired → recipient) into the KV list `notif:<user>` (30 newest) with `notif:<user>:seen` marking what is read; `GET/POST /api/notifications` (session only) feed the **bell** in `SiteNav` (`NotificationBell`; every `notify()` and mark-as-read bumps `notif:<user>:v`, the bell polls `GET /api/notifications?since=<v>` which answers 204 when nothing changed: every 10s while the tab is visible, 60s after 5 minutes without input, not while hidden, at once on focus or after a trade action (`tcg:notices-check` event from `postJson`). A new notice toasts (portal on `<body>`), shakes the bell, prefixes the tab title with `(N)` and sets the app badge; `UserSettings.noticeSound` adds a chime. **Web Push** (ROADMAP 4.3): `notify()` also calls `pushNotice()` (`lib/push.ts`, the only importer of `web-push`; pure shapes and validation in `lib/push-core.ts`, browser half in `lib/push-client.ts`), which sends through `after()` when VAPID keys are set, not in a sandbox, the bell switch (`wantsNotice`) allows the notice and `UserSettings.pushKinds` (offers / results / watch / admin, all on by default) does too. Devices are the KV hash `push:<user>` (max 5, oldest dropped; a 404 / 410 from the push service removes one); `GET/POST/DELETE /api/account/push` (session only, `isSameOrigin`). Requests are signed with `generateRequestDetails` and sent with `fetch`, so a plain-http localhost endpoint works outside production for `scripts/mock-push.cjs` / `scripts/check-push.mts`. `public/sw.js` shows the alert and opens `data.url` on click; Settings -> Notifications (`PushAlerts`) and a once-only toast offer in `NotificationBell` (`localStorage` `tcg_push_offered`, only after an offer or counter arrives) turn it on. Without `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` / `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` the whole option is hidden. Pure rules in `lib/notice-poll-core.ts`, `scripts/check-notice-poll.mts`). The engine queues events while it holds the trade lock and sends them after it is released (`queueEvent` / `flushEvents` in `lib/trades.ts`; building them reads the season, which must not hold the lock). Add new outcomes through `closeTrade` / `createInLock` so they are announced. **Trade animation** (`TradeCeremony.tsx`, `.tr-*` CSS): plays for the accepter when `POST /api/trades/[id]` returns `swap`, and for the offerer when the bell finds a fresh `accepted` notice (once, remembered in `localStorage` `tcg_trade_played`; "Replay" in the bell). Cards keep their holo (`SwapCard.holo`, via `buildSwap`). **Activity / Discord**: every action (offered, countered, accepted, declined, cancelled, expired) is a `trade` activity event carrying both sides' cards as readable lines plus points, the note and kind; Discord posts offers, counters, completed trades and admin cancels (not declines / withdrawals / expiries) as an embed with a field per side. The card popup on someone else's collection has an **Offer a trade** button (`/trade/new?to=<user>&want=<card>.<variant>` pre-selects the card you ask for). Sandbox-testable like everything on `kv`. `scripts/check-trades.mts` (needs the mock KV) checks conservation, races, expiry, caps, bulk cancel / extend and the minimum account age. **Admin Trades page**: `lib/trade-insights.ts` (client-safe, pure) computes the stats (`computeTradeStats`, from open + the kept 400-trade history, so it describes recent activity) and the flags (`flagTrades`: lopsided = one side ≥ 2× the other and ≥ 50 points apart at today's values, repeat pair = 3+ completed trades between two viewers in 7 days, rapid offers = 5+ in 10 minutes, new account = profile under 24h old when offered; thresholds in `FLAG_RULES`). Flags are advisory; "Block from trading" uses the Users moderation flag (`saveFlags`) and cancels that viewer's open offers. `TradeSettings.minAccountHours` (0 = off) stops a profile younger than that offering or accepting. Bulk engine functions `cancelOpenTrades` / `extendOpenTrades` run in one locked pass and announce through `closeTrade`.
- **Listing alerts (watches)**: `UserSettings.watches` (up to `WATCH_MAX` = 10 `{ card, variant | null }`, "tell me when someone lists a Shiny Gouda"; edited in Settings -> Saved alerts, anchor `#watches`) plus the existing Looking for list (any version) are the viewer's alerts; `notifyWatch` (default on) switches the notices off (`'watch'` notice group). Pure `lib/watch-core.ts` (`matchingAlerts`, `alertCards`, `watchLabel`); server `lib/watch-index.ts`: a KV set per card number `watch:idx:<card>` of the viewers who want it, kept up to date by `saveUserSettings` (`syncWatchIndex`) and built on first use from the viewers who have played (`watch:idx:ready`; no key scan, sandboxes cannot scan). A new board **listing** (not a direct offer or counter) goes through `announceTrade` -> `notifyWatchers` (lib/trade-notify.ts): every matching viewer except the lister gets one `watch` bell notice (cap 200 per listing; bell link "See the listing" -> `/trade?tab=board`). The website's Matches panel treats a watched card as wanted (card-level; a variant-specific watch also matches other versions there). **Sanitiser gotcha**: `sanitizeUserSettings` must give every array a fresh copy (`experiments`, `watches`), never push into the shared `DEFAULT_SETTINGS` ones. **Wanted counts** (ROADMAP 6.1): for each card an open board listing gives, `wantedCounts` (pure, `lib/watch-core.ts`) counts the people on that card's watch list minus the lister and any recipient; `wantedForTrades` (`lib/watch-index.ts`) reads them in one batch for the visible listings. Shown as a purple "N want this" chip in `TradeStacks` (`wanted` prop through `TradeCard`) on `/trade` and `/trade/<id>`, and as a "Wanted" line in the Trade listings Discord embed. Counts only, never names. `scripts/check-watch.mts` (mock KV).
- **Trade page upgrades**: `/trade` has `?tab=` sections (**Your trades** with offers for you, your open offers and **Matches for you**; **The board**; **History**; the default is Your trades when you have open offers, else the board). **Matches** come from the pure `lib/trade-match.ts` (`matchTrade`, `bestMatches`): board listings you can take that give a card on your Looking for list or one you do not own at all, or ask only for your spares (a spare = you would still keep a copy) at no loss, ranked with plain-language reasons. The board has `BoardToolbar` (search a card or collector; I can take / Has a card I want / Has a card I lack / Asks for my spares; sort by newest, ending soon or best for me): listings opt in with `data-board` + `data-*` attributes and the toolbar toggles `hidden` and CSS `order`, no re-render. The composer (`TradeComposer`) adds **quick picks** (cards the other person is looking for that you own, and cards you are looking for that they have; their list comes back from `GET /api/trades/holdings` as `wanted`), a **Spares only** filter on your side and a **fairness label** from `fairness()`; a lopsided offer (you give at least twice as many points and 50+ more) needs a tick before it can be sent. `scripts/check-trade-match.mts` covers the matching and fairness rules. The engine (escrow, locks, serials, notices) is unchanged.
- **Composer states (`/trade/new`)**: a message page (no season, frozen season, trading paused, **trading switched off for this viewer**, a counter of an offer that is gone or **not addressed to the viewer**) keeps the heading (`say()` in the page). A link that names the other person (`?to=`, `?counter=`, `?want=`) loads their cards at once (`TradeComposer` runs `lookUp()` on mount when `initial.to` is set).
- **Second tap on every action (`TradeActions`)**: Accept / Take this trade / Decline / Cancel offer each need a second tap, like the Twitch panel: the first tap turns the button into "Sure?" with a short "Tap again to ..." line and it relaxes after 4 seconds; each offer card keeps its own armed state. Used on `/trade` and on the profile Trades tab.
- **Trade page (`/trade/[id]`, in the `(entity)` group: real 404, noindex)**: one offer on its own address, the link Discord listing posts use. It runs `sweepTrades()`, reads the open trade or its history entry, and shows `TradeCard` (stacks with serials, lister, expiry) plus, for the signed-in viewer, a fairness line from their side and the same `TradeActions` second-tap flow (lister: Cancel offer; recipient of a direct offer: Accept / Counter / Decline; anyone else on a listing: Take this trade). Signed out: "Log in with Twitch" with `returnTo` back here. A **direct offer is private**: `tradeVisibleTo(trade, viewer)` (lib/trade-core.ts) lets only its two people see it, everyone else gets "This is a private offer". A closed trade shows its outcome and no actions. Board and Matches cards link to it ("Open").
- **Listing posts in Discord**: a new board listing (not a direct offer or counter) also calls `notifyListing()` (lib/discord.ts) from `announceTrade`, for channels with **Trade listings** on: "<lister> listed a trade", Gives (with the edition numbers of the copies that move) / Wants, points, an end time (`<t:...:R>`), the first card's art, and the title linking to `/trade/<id>`. Mentions stay empty and the post is never edited when the listing is taken or ends (the trade page shows the outcome). Silent in a sandbox.
- **Choosing which print to trade (ROADMAP 6.4)**: `TradeItem.ids?` (copy ids, `ids.length === count`, all distinct and of that card + version; `cleanIds` / `cleanItems` in `lib/trade-core.ts`) names exact prints; absent = any print, the newest first, exactly as before (old trades, the Twitch panel and unpicked asks all keep working). **Give side**: `createInLock` checks inside the collection lock that every id is held by the offerer right now (`firstNotHeld`, `lib/serials.ts`: not sold, forged, or already in another offer's escrow) and escrow / settle / refund pass `moveOf(item)` (`moves[].ids`) so the same prints go out and come back. **Get side**: a direct offer may pin the recipient's prints (checked held by them at offer time, and for a counter also in the escrow of the offer it answers; a **listing refuses ids on `get`**, a stranger picks at accept). `acceptTrade(id, user, getIds?)` takes `getIds` (`POST /api/trades/[id]` accept body, `{ "<collection entry>": ["<print id>", ...] }`, the entry being `encodeEntry(card, variant)`); `withChosenPrints` fills the taker's picks into the stacks the offerer did not pin; every picked print is re-checked held by the taker inside the lock, and if one has moved the accept fails ("That offer asks for Print #1023381, which you no longer hold.") and the offer stays open. The accepted trade's `get` is rewritten with the prints that really moved. A season whose prints are not numbered (`isTracking` false) refuses ids. `stackPoints` (and so fairness, matches, Discord and feed points) prices a picked stack from its own ids, otherwise from the newest of the pool. UI: `GET /api/trades/holdings` also returns `prints` (a `PrintPool`: id, edition `n`, `uid` per entry, `getPrintPool` in `lib/trade-view.ts`); `PrintPicker` (chips, "Use the newest", an Edition #1 / edition-bonus warning when giving) is used by the composer ("Choose prints" on a stack with spare prints, default newest) and by `TradeActions` (`pick` prop from `pickPropsFor`; on the first Accept / Take tap it loads the viewer's prints and, when they have spares of an asked-for card, shows the picker before the second-tap "Sure?"). `TradeStacks` shows picked prints with "Print #uid" (`TradePools.uids`) and "any print" on an open trade's asking side. Twitch panel: unchanged (frozen); its serial preview can differ from what moves for a picked trade until the next extension version.

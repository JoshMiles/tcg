# Trading

> Read this when you touch trades, escrow, the trade pages, notifications, listing alerts or the Twitch panel Trades tab.

## Trading
- **Trading (website + the Twitch panel's Trades tab)**: direct offers to one viewer and an open board of listings, any copy in any variant. **Escrow**: offering removes the copies from the offerer's collection into the trade (`Trade.escrowed`), so collections + escrow is conserved; accept swaps (`settleTrade`), decline / cancel / expiry / counter refund. Escrowed copies don't count towards score while open. Storage in KV: `trades:open`, `trades:history` (capped 400), `trade:settings`, `trade:cd:<user>`. Pure logic in `lib/trade-core.ts` (client-safe), engine in `lib/trades.ts`, every mutation under `withLock('trades')` then a collection lock (always that order). **No background job**: expired offers are refunded lazily by `sweepTrades()`, called from `/trade`, `/trade/new`, `/account`, `/collection/[username]` and `/admin/trades`; call it from any new page that shows collections. The viewer always comes from the session (`requireViewer` in `lib/trade-api.ts`), never the body. API: `POST /api/trades`, `POST /api/trades/[id]` (`accept|decline|cancel|counter`), `GET /api/trades/holdings`. Pages: `/trade`, `/trade/new[?to=|?counter=]`, `/admin/trades` (settings, pause switch, cancelling still works while paused, , open offers with cancel & refund, log). Settings: `TradeSettings` (expiry 48h, 5 open offers, 20s cooldown, 8 stacks per side). **Notifications**: every outcome notifies whoever needs to know (`lib/trade-notify.ts`: new offer / counter → recipient; accepted / declined / admin cancel / expired → offerer; withdrawn / expired → recipient) into the KV list `notif:<user>` (30 newest) with `notif:<user>:seen` marking what is read; `GET/POST /api/notifications` (session only) feed the **bell** in `SiteNav` (`NotificationBell`, polls every 45s and on tab focus). The engine queues events while it holds the trade lock and sends them after it is released (`queueEvent` / `flushEvents` in `lib/trades.ts`; building them reads the season, which must not hold the lock). Add new outcomes through `closeTrade` / `createInLock` so they are announced. **Trade animation** (`TradeCeremony.tsx`, `.tr-*` CSS): plays for the accepter when `POST /api/trades/[id]` returns `swap`, and for the offerer when the bell finds a fresh `accepted` notice (once, remembered in `localStorage` `tcg_trade_played`; "Replay" in the bell). Cards keep their holo (`SwapCard.holo`, via `buildSwap`). **Activity / Discord**: every action (offered, countered, accepted, declined, cancelled, expired) is a `trade` activity event carrying both sides' cards as readable lines plus points, the note and kind; Discord posts offers, counters, completed trades and admin cancels (not declines / withdrawals / expiries) as an embed with a field per side. The card popup on someone else's collection has an **Offer a trade** button (`/trade/new?to=<user>&want=<card>.<variant>` pre-selects the card you ask for). Sandbox-testable like everything on `kv`. `scripts/check-trades.mts` (needs the mock KV) checks conservation, races, expiry, caps, bulk cancel / extend and the minimum account age. **Admin Trades page**: `lib/trade-insights.ts` (client-safe, pure) computes the stats (`computeTradeStats`, from open + the kept 400-trade history, so it describes recent activity) and the flags (`flagTrades`: lopsided = one side ≥ 2× the other and ≥ 50 points apart at today's values, repeat pair = 3+ completed trades between two viewers in 7 days, rapid offers = 5+ in 10 minutes, new account = profile under 24h old when offered; thresholds in `FLAG_RULES`). Flags are advisory; "Block from trading" uses the Users moderation flag (`saveFlags`) and cancels that viewer's open offers. `TradeSettings.minAccountHours` (0 = off) stops a profile younger than that offering or accepting. Bulk engine functions `cancelOpenTrades` / `extendOpenTrades` run in one locked pass and announce through `closeTrade`.
- **Listing alerts (watches)**: `UserSettings.watches` (up to `WATCH_MAX` = 10 `{ card, variant | null }`, "tell me when someone lists a Shiny Gouda"; edited in Settings -> Saved alerts, anchor `#watches`) plus the existing Looking for list (any version) are the viewer's alerts; `notifyWatch` (default on) switches the notices off (`'watch'` notice group). Pure `lib/watch-core.ts` (`matchingAlerts`, `alertCards`, `watchLabel`); server `lib/watch-index.ts`: a KV set per card number `watch:idx:<card>` of the viewers who want it, kept up to date by `saveUserSettings` (`syncWatchIndex`) and built on first use from the viewers who have played (`watch:idx:ready`; no key scan, sandboxes cannot scan). A new board **listing** (not a direct offer or counter) goes through `announceTrade` -> `notifyWatchers` (lib/trade-notify.ts): every matching viewer except the lister gets one `watch` bell notice (cap 200 per listing; bell link "See the listing" -> `/trade?tab=board`). The website's Matches panel treats a watched card as wanted (card-level; a variant-specific watch also matches other versions there). **Sanitiser gotcha**: `sanitizeUserSettings` must give every array a fresh copy (`experiments`, `watches`), never push into the shared `DEFAULT_SETTINGS` ones. `scripts/check-watch.mts` (mock KV).
- **Trade page upgrades**: `/trade` has `?tab=` sections (**Your trades** with offers for you, your open offers and **Matches for you**; **The board**; **History**; the default is Your trades when you have open offers, else the board). **Matches** come from the pure `lib/trade-match.ts` (`matchTrade`, `bestMatches`): board listings you can take that give a card on your Looking for list or one you do not own at all, or ask only for your spares (a spare = you would still keep a copy) at no loss, ranked with plain-language reasons. The board has `BoardToolbar` (search a card or collector; I can take / Has a card I want / Has a card I lack / Asks for my spares; sort by newest, ending soon or best for me): listings opt in with `data-board` + `data-*` attributes and the toolbar toggles `hidden` and CSS `order`, no re-render. The composer (`TradeComposer`) adds **quick picks** (cards the other person is looking for that you own, and cards you are looking for that they have; their list comes back from `GET /api/trades/holdings` as `wanted`), a **Spares only** filter on your side and a **fairness label** from `fairness()`; a lopsided offer (you give at least twice as many points and 50+ more) needs a tick before it can be sent. `scripts/check-trade-match.mts` covers the matching and fairness rules. The engine (escrow, locks, serials, notices) is unchanged.

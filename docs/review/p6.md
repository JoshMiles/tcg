# Review batch P6: Viewer account

Pages: `/account` (My Stats: score, rank, What to do next, points breakdown, Ready to upgrade with the upgrade ceremony, completion by rarity, most valuable copies, versions, awards, season picker, Share my stats), `/settings` (every section, Your data export), the Twitch login (`/auth/twitch`, its callback, `/auth/logout`), `/auth/error` and `/login` (the staff sign-in). Reviewed 2026-10-15 against the seeded review data (`docs/review.md`).

## Method

- Sweep (`node scripts/sweep-public.mjs --as=<viewer> /account ...`) as `alice` (a full collection), `tester` (a new viewer) and `upgrade_ready` (a viewer with upgrades available), every page above plus an unknown season, every login error, at 390, 768 and 1280 px: clean.
- Hand pass with screenshots: My Stats for a full collection, for a viewer with an upgrade, for a new viewer; Settings; the staff sign-in with an error; the login-cancelled page.
- Interaction checks: an upgrade end to end (one click, the server upgrades and the list refreshes: the new Reverse Holo copy got serial #17 with its x1.4 bonus); the ended-season look back (no upgrade buttons); Settings (typing marks "Unsaved changes", Discard restores, the bio is cut at 160, Save persists across a reload, a Looking for card is added, the data export downloads `cheesy-tcg-<name>.json`, a private profile then reads "This profile is private" to a visitor); the login flow (a bad `state` or no cookie goes to `/auth/error?reason=expired`, a cancelled login to `reason=denied`, `returnTo` values pointing off the site or at `javascript:` fall back to `/account`, `/admin` for a non-staff account lands on the staff sign-in with "not on the staff list"); logout (POST only, refused without a same-origin `Origin`); the staff password form (a wrong password shows the error, a right one signs in).

## Findings

### Bugs (fixed)

- **The keyboard-shortcuts description in Settings was wrong**: it said `c` jumps to "Collection" and left out `e`. The real keys are `c` = My profile and `e` = Settings (and S is Season stats, not Stats). The text now names every key and destination, and `scripts/check-user-settings.mts` fails if a key or label in `PUBLIC_GO_KEYS` is missing from it.
- **My Stats, looking back at a season you had no cards in** said "Your collection is empty. Watch the stream, cards are pulled live" for an ended season. It now says "You had no cards in <season>" and that the season is over (the current-season text is unchanged).

### Visual / accessibility (fixed)

- The staff sign-in's password field was not focused after opening "Use the admin password instead"; it is now.

### Improvements (for you to pick)

1. **Upgrade (5) on My Stats upgrades on one click.** The page says upgrades are permanent and the copies you spend are destroyed, yet a single tap does it (the trade actions got a second tap in P5 for the same reason). A "Sure?" second tap would match.
2. **"Your score over time" shows nothing for most of a viewer's first day.** It needs two samples, taken at most every 30 minutes and only when the score or rank changed or six hours passed; until then the section is simply missing (the "starts from your first visit" note only appears together with the chart). A one-line placeholder for the first day would explain the gap.

Nothing found.

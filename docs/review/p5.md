# Review batch P5: Trading

Pages: `/trade` (Your trades with offers for you, your open offers and Matches; The board with its toolbar; History), `/trade/new` (the composer: to one person / on the board, counter, `?to=`, `?want=`), the trade animation and the notification bell. Reviewed 2026-10-15 against the seeded review data (`docs/review.md`).

## Method

- Sweep (`node scripts/sweep-public.mjs --as=<viewer> /trade ... /trade/new?counter=...`) as `trader_b` (has an offer waiting), `alice` (made offers) and `blocked_bob` (trading switched off), signed in and out, at 390, 768 and 1280 px. First run flagged `h1=0` on the composer's error states; clean after the fixes.
- Hand pass with screenshots: Your trades with a waiting offer and a Matches card, the board with its toolbar, the composer at 1280 and 390 px, History, the bell with a declined notice, the trade animation.
- End-to-end flows in a browser on the seeded data: `alice` composed and sent a direct offer to `trader_a` from `?to=`; `trader_a` saw it under Offers for you and declined it; `alice` got a bell notice ("declined your offer, your cards are back in your collection") and cancelled another offer; History then listed both as Declined and Cancelled; `trader_b` took `trader_a`'s board listing (Take this trade), the "Trade complete!" animation played for them, it closes with Esc after its short hold, and `trader_a` got the accepted notice and the animation played once (remembered in `localStorage`, no replay on reload).
- States: a season that has ended (`?season=season1`: "Trading is closed for ...", open offers can still be cancelled), a viewer with trading switched off, a counter of an offer that is gone or not yours, signed out.

## Findings

### Bugs (fixed)

- **Composer messages had no heading** ("No season is running", "Trading is closed", "Trading is paused", "That offer is no longer open"): the page then had no `h1`. They now carry a (screen-reader) "New trade offer" heading.
- **Countering an offer that was not made to you said "That offer is no longer open"**, which is false: it is open, just not yours. It now says "That offer wasn't made to you, so you can't counter it."; the gone / already-answered case keeps the old text.
- **A viewer with trading switched off got the full composer** and only learned when they pressed Send ("Trading isn't available on your account"). The composer page now says so up front.
- **`/trade/new?to=<name>` (the "Trade with ..." button on a profile) did not load their cards**: the name was filled in but the right-hand column said "Look the person up to see what they have" until you pressed Find their cards. A link that names the person now loads their cards straight away (counters and offers started from a card already did).

### Improvements (for you to pick)

1. **Accept, Take this trade, Decline and Cancel act on a single click on the website** (the Twitch panel asks "Sure?" first). Accepting swaps cards for good, so a stray tap on a phone cannot be undone. A second tap or a confirmation for Accept / Take (and Cancel / Decline if you want them consistent) would match the panel.
2. **Signed out, the empty board says "Post a listing and let others come to you"** although you cannot post until you log in (the page does say "Log in with Twitch to trade" above it). A signed-out empty state could point at logging in instead.
3. **The bell opens over the page content without dimming it and has no "mark all read" or "clear"**; only the 30 newest notices are kept. Probably fine, noted for the record.

### Panel

The panel's Trades tab (freeze applies) asks for a second tap before Accept / Decline / Cancel; the website does not. Logged for Phase 4 parity, not changed here.

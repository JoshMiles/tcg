Pages: `/u/[username]` (header; Overview widgets and Customize; Collection grid, card dialog and binder; Stats; Trades; Activity; Share) and the `/collection/[username]` redirect. Reviewed 2026-10-15 against the seeded review data (`docs/review.md`).

## Method

- Sweep (`node scripts/sweep-public.mjs --as=alice /u/alice ...`, every tab, `hoarder` with 3,000 copies, the private / hidden / long-named / empty / unknown viewers, the redirect) at 390, 768 and 1280 px, signed in as the owner and out: clean.
- Hand pass with screenshots: owner and visitor Overview at 1280 px, Customize profile mode, a new viewer's empty profile, the long-name header at 390 px, the Collection tab as a visitor comparing with their own cards, the card dialog, the binder cover, the Share tab, a Trades tab with an open listing.

## Findings

### Bugs (fixed)

- **A draft pack attached to a published season made the season impossible to complete and diluted every card.** `getSeasonLeaderboard` read every pack of the season, drafts included, although a draft is never drawn and nobody can own its cards. With a 5-card draft pack next to a 20-card live pack the set was 25 cards: a viewer holding all 20 drawable cards was not `complete` (no Complete chip, no complete-set bonus, no Completionist award), and every card's pull rate, rarity and value were computed against cards that cannot exist (one card was worth 4 points in every version instead of 77 / 465 / 1,673 / 7,744). Drafts are now left out (`lib/leaderboard.ts`, also the scoring preview in Settings and the profile share image's card total); an inactive but published pack still counts, because its cards can be held. The achievements, the wrap-up and the admin dashboard already left drafts out. `scripts/check-draft-packs.mts` covers it. **This changes scores for any season that has a draft pack attached**; with none attached nothing changes.
- **The profile header showed the viewer's numeric Twitch ID** ("@alice · Twitch: 100510") to everyone, on every profile, whenever it differed from the login (always, for real accounts). It is not needed and is not something the Privacy Policy lists as displayed; removed.

### Visual / copy (fixed)

- The Collection tab's **Show** filter listed "Collected" or "Everything" twice (the default entry repeated the explicit ones). The default entry now reads "Default (collected)" / "Default (everything)".

### Improvements (all three done afterwards, see the follow-up at the end)

1. **Tab titles are inconsistent**: "Alice's profile", "Alice's collection", "Alice · Stats", "Alice · Trades", "Alice · Activity". One pattern would read better in browser tabs and shared links ("Alice · Stats" everywhere, or "Alice's stats"). Touches `generateMetadata` in `app/u/[username]/page.tsx` and `scripts/check-public-meta.mts`.
2. **Private profiles still show everything on the page** (bio, cards, a Trade button) to anyone with the link; the setting only removes the viewer from boards and feeds, which the Settings page and FAQ say. If "private" should mean "hide the page", that is a product decision (planning).
3. **Rarity progress** only lists tiers the season has cards in, so a season with no 1-star cards shows four tiles and a wide empty strip on the right; the tiles could be centred or span the width.

Nothing found.

## Follow-up: the three improvements, done (2026-10-15)

1. **Titles**: one pattern for every tab, "Alice's profile", "Alice's collection", "Alice's stats", "Alice's trades", "Alice's activity", "Alice's share" (`profileTitle` in `lib/profile-url.ts`, checked in `scripts/check-profile.mts`).
2. **Private profiles now hide the page.** Anyone but the owner who opens `/u/<name>` of a private profile (any tab, signed in or out) sees "This profile is private" with a link to Find a collector; the tab title is "Private profile", the page is `noindex, nofollow`, and the link-preview image carries no name, rank or progress. The owner still sees everything. The admin-set "hide from boards" flag is unchanged (that page still opens). Settings help, the FAQ and the Privacy Policy say so. Not changed: a private viewer's open listings on the trade board still show their name and link to the (now private) page, and the Twitch panel's collection endpoint still answers by username (panel freeze).
3. **Rarity tiles** (Overview widget and the Collection tab) now fill the row whatever the number of tiers.

# Review batch P1: Front door

Pages: `/` home, the top bar (search, rank chip, bell, account menu, live pill, install icon), the footer, `/collection`, `/install`, `/offline`, the 404 / error / loading states and the cookie notice. Reviewed 2026-10-15 against the seeded review data (`docs/review.md`).

## Method

- Sweep (`node scripts/sweep-public.mjs / /collection /install /offline /does-not-exist /u/nobody_here /auth/error?reason=denied`) at 390, 768 and 1280 px, signed in and out: first run flagged one problem (top bar overflow at 768 px signed in); clean after the fixes.
- Hand pass with screenshots at 390 and 1280 px: home signed out, as a collector (`alice`), as a new viewer (`tester`) and with open trade offers (`trader_b`); `/collection`, `/install`, the 404, the account menu, the bell, the search dialog, the mobile menu.
- Interaction checks: the cookie notice (shows, dismissal survives navigation, footer "Cookies" reopens it); the account menu by keyboard (opens focused, arrows move, Esc closes and returns focus); the mobile menu (Esc closes, focus returns to the burger); the LIVE pill and banner (with the fake Twitch channel live); the search API leaves out admin-hidden and private collectors; home with no running season.
- Top bar overflow probe at 1024 / 1100 / 1180 / 1280 / 1440 / 1920 px for signed out, a collector and a staff admin (the three header layouts), 18 combinations.

## Findings

### Bugs (fixed)

- **No season running, last season's podium never shown.** The home page promises a "How <season> ended" podium when no season is running, but it only looked for one when there were no seasons at all (`!season`), and the site falls back to showing the latest season. With Season 2 published but not active, the podium for the season that just ended was missing. Now shown whenever the shown season is not active (`app/(site)/page.tsx`).

### Visual (fixed)

- **Top bar overflowed the page** (the whole page scrolled sideways) at 768 px signed in (144 px), at 1024 px for staff (42 px) and for collectors (9 px), and at 1024 px signed out (53 px). Cause: the desktop links, search, LIVE pill, bell and account chip all showed from `md`. Now: the links and the burger menu switch at `xl` (1280 px), the Search label and `/` hint show from `2xl`, the staff panel button and the display name show from `2xl` (the staff link and the name stay in the account menu). Checked clean at all 18 width and role combinations above.
- **Nav links and the Log in button wrapped onto two lines** at 1280 px while the LIVE pill was showing ("Season Stats", "Card Dex", "Log in with Twitch" each broke). The links and the button are now `whitespace-nowrap`.

### Accessibility (fixed)

- The account menu button lost its text label when the name is hidden at narrower widths; it now has `aria-label="Account menu, <name>"`.

### Improvements (for you to pick)

1. **Nav says "Collections" but the page is "Find a collector"** (title, heading and route `/collection`). Rename the nav and footer label to "Collectors" so they agree.
2. **Site search matches cards by scattered letters**: typing `ali` returns the card "Goatlygidds" (letters a, l, i appear in order). `docs/public-site.md` says only pages avoid scattered matching; for cards and collectors a prefix or word match would be less noisy. Touches `lib/site-search.ts` (`rankSearch`) and its check.
3. **Home: Top collectors (5 rows) is shorter than Recent pulls (8 rows)**, leaving empty space under the list on desktop. Show 8 collectors, or let the list grow with the pulls column.
4. **Hero decoration**: the faint cheese-hole circle in the bottom left of the hero sits under the "Leaderboard" quick link. Harmless, but moving it a little would tidy the corner.

### Panel

Nothing found (the panel is out of scope here and under the freeze).

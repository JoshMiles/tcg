# Review batch A3: packs and cards

Pages: `/admin/packs` (library, toolbar), `/admin/packs/new` (wizard, resume a draft), `/admin/packs/[packId]` (Cards, Odds & simulator, Overview, Settings), `.../cards` (bulk editor), `.../cards/[cardId]` (card editor). Seeded with `scripts/seed-review.mts`, swept with `scripts/sweep-admin.mjs` at 390, 768 and 1280 px, then walked by hand in a browser.

## Sweep

All pack and card pages clean at the three widths. Unknown pack and card ids answer 200 with the shared admin not-found screen (no `h1`; left for A8). The bulk editor and card editor had no page title of their own ("The Cheesy TCG").

## Bugs (fixed)

1. **The pack Settings form and the card forms trusted the browser.** Reproduced before the fix: a card saved with a **duplicate number** (two cards sharing every viewer's copies), an **empty name**, **9 stars** and a **negative probability**; the pack settings accepted a blank name, a negative draw weight, holo stars of 9, and a **volume of 0 could not be saved** (it silently became 1, so a pack could not be muted although the field says "0-1" and the wizard allows it). The wizard's autosave already cleaned its input; the Settings and card forms did not. The cleaner moved to a pure `lib/pack-clean.ts` (`cleanPackInput`, `cardProblems`), and `updatePack`, `addCard` and `updateCard` now use it on the server. A problem sends the admin back with a red "Not saved: ..." banner and stores nothing; numbers are brought inside their limits (volume 0-1, whole upgrade costs of at least 1, holo stars 1-5). Card numbers must be whole, below 1,000,000 (the variant offset) and unique within the pack. `scripts/check-pack-clean.mts` covers the rules and that the actions use them.
2. **Dead `createPack` action removed.** Nothing called it; it was a public server action that made a pack with none of those checks.
3. **The pack wizard had the season wizard's bugs.** "Cancel" on a resumed draft deleted it (after a browser confirm), and discarding right after an edit let the pending autosave fire at the deleted draft. Cancel is only shown before a draft exists, Discard draft is a two-click button, and the discard flushes the autosave first.

## Visuals and accessibility (fixed)

- Card editor: the editor says "N things to fix before this will save" for the problems the server now refuses (name, number, stars, odds) and keeps "you can still save" for the softer ones; the number, stars and probability inputs carry their limits.
- Form labels tied to their inputs (`for` / `id`) in the pack settings (`PackFields`), the card editor, the Add card dialog and the pack wizard; the pack settings number fields carry min / max.
- Tab titles name the pack and the card; the bulk editor has a title.
- Move to drafts (pack page) is a confirmation that says when it stops being drawn on stream; the bulk editor's per-card delete is a two-click button ("Sure?") instead of `window.confirm`; the wizard's discard likewise.
- The delete-pack dialogs (library and pack page) name the seasons the pack is used in and say viewers' scores change.

## Checked and fine

Library search, status filters and empty message, odds panel (normalize, simulate, save disabled when clean), the odds action's own validation, bulk-editor row delete staging, duplicate (lands in the wizard as a draft), the Delete pack dialog, move to drafts, the card editor's live preview and checks.

## Left for planning

- **The card editor lets you change a card's number**, while the bulk editor says "Card numbers never change once assigned" and viewers' collections point at numbers. Renumbering a card that has been pulled hands every viewer's copies to whatever card holds the new number. The server now refuses duplicates and bad numbers, but whether a number should be locked once the card has been pulled is a data-model decision.
- **Deleting a pack or a card that viewers have pulled** leaves their collection entries pointing at nothing. The dialogs now say so; archiving (which keeps the cards) is the safe route, and the library already offers it.

## Found for later batches

`window.confirm` is still used for destructive actions in `StaffManager` (A4), `UserPanels` restore point and merge (A4) and `TradeControls` bulk cancel (A5). Each should become a `ConfirmButton` or `ArmedButton`.

title: How to restrict a viewer
summary: Which switch to use, what to write in the reason, and who you cannot restrict.
order: 60
audience: mods
---

Open the viewer from **Viewers** (or from a report in Feedback), then use the restrictions box on their page.

## The two switches

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Block trading | They cannot make or accept offers, and nobody can offer to them. Their open offers can still be cancelled. |
| Hide from public boards | They drop out of the leaderboard, the card owner lists and recent pulls. Their own collection page still works. |

Use Block trading for trade abuse. Use Hide from public boards for names or profiles that should not be on show. Both can be on at once.

## Steps

1. Look at their recent activity and trades first, so you know what happened.
2. Flip the switch.
3. Write a reason. Only staff see it, and the next person on shift reads it.
4. Press **Save restrictions**. Nothing applies until you do.

The page shows when it was set and by whom. To lift a restriction, flip the switch off and save again.

## Limits

- You cannot restrict yourself, other staff, or the streamer's account.
- You cannot change a restriction an admin set. Ask an admin.
- Their open offers stay open. Cancel them from the viewer's Trades list so the cards go back to their owner.

If you are unsure, restrict only what stops the problem and tell an admin. See "What to escalate to an admin".

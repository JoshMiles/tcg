Tell an admin (or the streamer) in your usual staff chat, with the viewer's name and what you saw.

## Always escalate

- **Deletion requests.** Only admins answer them.
- **Missing cards.** Mods cannot grant, take or restore cards. Say which viewer, which card and what they were doing.
- **A trade you cannot reverse**, or one that is too old to reverse automatically.
- **Cards that look stuck** after an offer ended.
- **A restriction an admin set** that you think is wrong.
- **Anything about other staff or the streamer's account.** You cannot moderate them.
- **Threats or anything outside the game.** Report it on Twitch too.

## Escalate if unsure

- You think an account is a copy of a restricted one.
- A viewer disputes your restriction and you cannot settle it in a message.
- Several accounts are doing the same thing at once.

## What to include

The viewer's login, the time, what happened, what you already did, and a link to the trade or the viewer page.

## You can handle yourself

Restricting a viewer, cancelling an open offer, reversing a clear mistake, and answering feedback. See the other guide topics.
